- 2026-10-22 · Legislation · Digital Omnibus: [Digital Omnibus: Mandate for negotiations with the European Parliament](../procedures/digital-omnibus.html). ST 14756/26 of 22 October 2026 is a note of the General Secretariat of the Council with the text of the mandate for negotiations with the European Parliament on the Digital Omnibus, as agreed by the Permanent Representatives Committee on 21 October 2026; it is a negotiating mandate, not an adopted act, and the text can still change in the negotiations. *What it means:* Against the Presidency compromise text of 3 September 2026 (ST 12535/26) the incident reporting provisions (information point at ENISA, national entry points) are essentially unchanged, while the recitals on funding and design of the national entry points are adjusted. The new wording concerns mainly the Data Act (rules on refusing access to trade secrets, with notification to the authority every three months and Commission guidelines), the GDPR (pseudonymisation, AI processing, data subject requests), the ePrivacy Directive (exceptions and consent rules for terminal equipment) and the application dates. None of the tracked concepts is touched.
