- 2026-10-06 · Legislation · NSR: [Commission proposes a new Standardisation Regulation](../acts/standardisation-revision.html). COM(2026) 780 would replace Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012: deadlines for every standardisation request, interim deliverables with a presumption of conformity, designated standards bodies besides CEN, CENELEC and ETSI when they are late, and free online access to every cited standard. *What it means:* Nothing changes for the standards manufacturers use today: M/606 for the CRA expires in November 2027 and runs under the current rules. The proposal matters for the second phase of machinery standards (M/605, due 2034), which would run across the switch without a transitional rule, and for interim deliverables, which the CRA and the Machinery Regulation do not yet recognise for the presumption of conformity.
- 2026-10-05 · Legislation · Digital Omnibus: [Digital Omnibus: Council mandate for talks with Parliament revised (not public)](../procedures/digital-omnibus.html). The register lists a revision (REV 1) of 5 October of the draft mandate of 2 October; neither is public yet. Coreper has to agree a mandate before trilogues can start. *What it means:* Until Coreper agrees the mandate and the Council publishes it, the Council's position on the Digital Omnibus is not officially known. Trilogues can start only after that.
- 2026-10-06 · Legislation · EPA: [European Product Act proposal postponed to later in 2026](../index.html#act=epa). Planned for 6 October 2026 together with the Standardisation Regulation; the Commission now expects to present it later this year, with the changes to sector legislation. Date to be confirmed. *What it means:* For products nothing changes now: Decision 768/2008/EC, Regulation (EC) No 765/2008 and Regulation (EU) 2019/1020 stay in force. The Standardisation Regulation goes ahead on its own for the time being.
