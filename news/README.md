- 2026-10-06 · Legislation · New Standardisation Regulation: [Commission proposes a new Standardisation Regulation](../acts/standardisation-revision.html). COM(2026) 780 would replace Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012: deadlines for every standardisation request, interim deliverables with a presumption of conformity, designated standards bodies besides CEN, CENELEC and ETSI when they are late, and free online access to every cited standard. *What it means:* Nothing changes for the standards manufacturers use today: M/606 for the CRA expires in November 2027 and runs under the current rules. The proposal matters for the second phase of machinery standards (M/605, due 2034), which would run across the switch without a transitional rule, and for interim deliverables, which the CRA and the Machinery Regulation do not yet recognise for the presumption of conformity.
- 2026-10-05 · Legislation · Digital Omnibus: [Digital Omnibus: Council mandate for talks with Parliament revised (not public)](../procedures/digital-omnibus.html). The register lists a revision (REV 1) of 5 October of the draft mandate of 2 October; neither is public yet. Coreper has to agree a mandate before trilogues can start. *What it means:* Until Coreper agrees the mandate and the Council publishes it, the Council's position on the Digital Omnibus is not officially known. Trilogues can start only after that.
- 2026-10-06 · Legislation · European Product Act: [European Product Act proposal postponed to later in 2026](../index.html#act=epa). Planned for 6 October 2026 together with the Standardisation Regulation; the Commission now expects to present it later this year, with the changes to sector legislation. Date to be confirmed. *What it means:* For products nothing changes now: Decision 768/2008/EC, Regulation (EC) No 765/2008 and Regulation (EU) 2019/1020 stay in force. The Standardisation Regulation goes ahead on its own for the time being.

- 2026-10-14 · Analysis · Site: [How our forecast is made, and how to read it](articles/forecast-method.html). When will a law that is still being negotiated be in the Official Journal? Our forecast answers with what comparable EU laws actually took, not with plans. This memo explains where it comes from, how it works, how often it was right in the past and what it cannot know.
- 2026-10-14 · Analysis · Site: [The forecast for statisticians: estimand, algorithm, evaluation and limits](articles/forecast-statistics.html). A technical note on our forecast of EU legislative procedures: a conditional, non-parametric reference class forecast with a size neighbourhood and a calendar transformation. Evaluated out of sample with PIT, interval coverage and the CRPS, including a check for look-ahead in our own backtest. What holds up, what does not, and where we would welcome advice.
- 2026-10-13 · Analysis · Site: [Large laws take longer: our forecast now compares like with like](articles/forecast-size.html). We tested which properties of a proposal go with how long the EU takes to adopt it. Size wins clearly; commas, consultations and the State of the Union address do not. Since 13 October the forecast compares each file with files of similar size, and most dates move later.
