## European Product Act: scenarios

- **One act**: The European Product Act as one act that merges the New Legislative Framework, the Market Surveillance Regulation and the Standardisation Regulation. The separate proposal for a New Standardisation Regulation of 6 October 2026 would then be folded into it. Official Journal: median Feb 2029, half of the runs Jul 2028 – Jan 2030; before the 2029 elections 60 %.
- **Package of two acts**: Two acts negotiated side by side, the New Standardisation Regulation (proposed on 6 October 2026) and one act on market surveillance and the New Legislative Framework. The package is complete when both are published. Official Journal: median Nov 2029, half of the runs Dec 2028 – Oct 2030; before the 2029 elections 43 %.

## Method

Reference class: 1007 COD files with trilogues proposed since 2012 (EP Legislative Observatory), of which the 40 % nearest in size (words of legal text of the Commission proposal) since 13 Oct 2026. Parliament's breaks around the elections of 2014, 2019 and 2024 are taken out of the durations, the break of 2029 is put into the forecast (assumed as in 2024). Track record, files proposed 2016-2021 (426): 49 % in the middle half, 80 % in the eight-in-ten range, median error 6.9 months. Next step on track until: the latest day the next step can come, from the day the current step was reached, without moving the median of publication by more than 14 days (close: fewer than 30 days left; close and late only when the next step is the committee vote). In the backtest (files proposed 2016-2021), files that missed their window finished a median 149 days later than those that met it.
