## European Product Act: scenarios

- **One act**: The European Product Act as one act that merges the New Legislative Framework, the Market Surveillance Regulation and the Standardisation Regulation. The separate proposal for a New Standardisation Regulation of 6 October 2026 would then be folded into it. Official Journal: median Sep 2028, half of the runs Feb 2028 – Nov 2029; before the 2029 elections 70 %.
- **Package of two acts**: Two acts negotiated side by side, the New Standardisation Regulation (proposed on 6 October 2026) and one act on market surveillance and the New Legislative Framework. The package is complete when both are published. Official Journal: median Mar 2029, half of the runs Aug 2028 – Apr 2030; before the 2029 elections 54 %.

## Method

Reference class: 906 COD files with trilogues proposed since 2012 (EP Legislative Observatory). Parliament's breaks around the elections of 2014, 2019 and 2024 are taken out of the durations, the break of 2029 is put into the forecast (assumed as in 2024). Track record, files proposed 2016-2021 (421): 52 % in the middle half, 75 % in the eight-in-ten range, median error 8.4 months.
