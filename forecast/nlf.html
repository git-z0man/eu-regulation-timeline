Product laws under the New Legislative Framework: changes in parallel

The European Product Act is to revise the framework that some thirty product laws build on, and the Commission intends to align those laws with it in a sectoral omnibus. But several of them are already being changed by other files, at different stages, at the same time. Which files change which law, from the titles of the proposals and acts:

What this cannot show. The European Product Act and the sectoral omnibus are the Commission's intention: it has not proposed them, and Parliament and Council have not given their views. Which product laws the omnibus will change is open; it is unlikely to change all of them. Pending files can change their scope in the negotiations, and a provision can change another act without naming it in the title.
