European Product Act

proposal assumed Dec 2026

When will it be law?

The European Product Act as one act that merges the New Legislative Framework, the Market Surveillance Regulation and the Standardisation Regulation. The separate proposal for a New Standardisation Regulation of 6 October 2026 would then be folded into it.

The package: one timetable for all its acts

Sectoral alignment omnibus: Changes to the sector laws, such as the Machinery Regulation, that align them with the European Product Act. The Commission intends to propose them together with it, so the forecast assumes the same day. Which of the product laws it will change is open, and Parliament and Council have not yet given their views.

All acts of the package published before the 2029 elections in 33 % of the runs. Each act follows its own runs; acts negotiated as one package move together and would finish closer to the latest proposal.

What the package view cannot show. The package is the Commission's plan. Parliament and Council have not given their views yet; they can split it, change its scope or take its acts at different speeds. The omnibus is unlikely to change every one of the some thirty product laws under the New Legislative Framework, and several of them are being amended in parallel by other files, such as the Omnibus IV acts on digitalisation and common specifications (13 directives and seven regulations, among them the Machinery Regulation) and the simplification of the medical devices rules.
