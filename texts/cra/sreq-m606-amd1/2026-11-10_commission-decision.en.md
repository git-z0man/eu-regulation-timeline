Whereas:

## Recitals

<a id="rec-1"></a>
(1) By Implementing Decision C(2025)618 ^(2), the Commission requested the European Committee for Standardisation (CEN), the European Committee for Electrotechnical Standardisation (Cenelec) and the European Telecommunications Standards Institute (ETSI) to draft new or revise existing European standards as regards products with digital elements in support of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 of the European Parliament and of the Council ^(3).

<a id="rec-2"></a>
(2) CEN, Cenelec and ETSI have accepted the standardisation request set out in Implementing Decision C(2025)618 and the standardisation work is ongoing.

<a id="rec-3"></a>
(3) In their joint semestrial report of 3 June 2026, CEN, Cenelec and ETSI informed the Commission about developments affecting the timing of certain activities. Taking into account the joint report and further interactions with the European Standardisation Organisations, the Commission considers that it is appropriate to slightly adjust the deadlines for 2026 for the finalisation of certain deliverables provided for in Annex I to Implementing Decision C(2025)618. That Implementing Decision should therefore be amended accordingly.

<a id="rec-4"></a>
(4) The harmonised standards adopted in response to standardisation request set out in Implementing Decision C(2025)618 as amended by this Decision may be subject to access to documents requests in accordance with Regulation (EC) 1049/2001 of the European Parliament and of the Council ^(4). In its judgement of 5 March 2024 C-588/21 P ^(5), the Court of Justice recognised that there is an overriding public interest, within the meaning of Article 4(2) of Regulation (EC) 1049/2001, justifying the disclosure of harmonised standards.

<a id="rec-5"></a>
(5) The European Standardisation Organisations and the Expert Group on cybersecurity of products with digital elements have been consulted.

<a id="rec-6"></a>
(6) The measures provided for in this Decision are in accordance with the opinion of the Committee established by Article 22 of Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

<a id="art-1"></a>
### Article 1

Annex I to Implementing Decision C(2025)618 is replaced by the text in the Annex to this Decision.

<a id="art-2"></a>
### Article 2

If the European Committee for Standardisation, the European Committee for Electrotechnical Standardisation or the European Telecommunications Standards Institute indicate that they do not accept the amendments set out in Article 1 of this Decision within a month of receiving it, this Decision shall cease to apply and Implementing Decision C(2025)618 in its version of 3 February 2025 shall continue to apply.

<a id="art-3"></a>
### Article 3

This Decision is addressed to the European Committee for Standardisation, the European Committee for Electrotechnical Standardisation and the European Telecommunications Standards Institute.

Done at Brussels, 10.11.2026

For the Commission Henna VIRKKUNEN Executive Vice-President
