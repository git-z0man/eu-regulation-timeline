WORKING DOCUMENT

From: General Secretariat of the Council To: Antici Group (Simplification)

Subject: Omnibus IV (Digitalisation and common specifications): Presidency compromise - AGS on 27 April 2026

Delegations will find enclosed a note by the Presidency presenting possible compromises with the European Parliament in light of the interinstitutional negotations held so far. The proposed compromises cover the issues of (i) common specifications - last resort clause, and (ii) digitalisation - request of instructions of use and safety information.

Delegations are invited to discuss these elements at the Antici Group (Simplifcation) meeting of 27 April, with a view to providing the necessary flexiblity to the Presidency in advancing interinstitutional negotations.

Delegations will find below Presidency compromise solutions on the issues of the last

resort clause and the request of use and safety information.

•  Regarding the last resort clause, the Presidency proposes to focus on the Council

red  lines  to  extend  the  transitional  measures  for  the  ESPR  and  to  delete  the

Accreditation Regulation, while in turn taking into account the  Parliament’s red

line of including a last resort clause, in a strictly circumscribed manner, ensuring

limited and proportionate use subject to effective control by the Council.

•  Regarding the request of use and safety information, the Presidency proposes to

focus on the Council’s stance of not  distinguishing between professional users

and consumers, while in turn taking into account the Parliament’s demands for

a 24-months delay, which aligns with a product guarantee period.

Delegations are invited to provide the Presidency with the necessary flexibility to advance

interinstitutional negotiations, on the understanding that concessions will be necessary

on  both  sides  to  achieve  a  swift  political  agreement  with  the  European  Parliament.

Delegations  are  encouraged,  beyond  sectoral  considerations,  to  consider  the  wider

simplification  endeavour  supported  as  a  matter  of  priority  by  EU  Leaders,  and  to  also

examine these proposals from a horizontal perspective.

PRESIDENCY  COMPROMISE  PROPOSAL  FOR  COMMON  SPECIFICATION  AS  A  LAST  RESORT CLAUSE

Last Resort Clause:

Proposed wording in paragraph 1a (new)

1a. In accordance with paragraph 1(a), where no harmonised standard exists enabling

compliance with the essential requirements set out in Annex II, and where no reference

to such a harmonised standard is expected to be published in the Official Journal of the

European Union within a reasonable period, the Commission may, as a measure of last

resort,  adopt  implementing  acts  establishing  common  specifications  to  address  the

exceptional situation in which the lack of cooperation with international standardisation

organisations prevents European standardisation organisations from developing relevant

harmonised  standards,  and  where  such  situation  cannot  be  adequately  mitigated  by

alternative measures.

Where the Commission considers that the conditions referred to in the first subparagraph

are  fulfilled,  it  shall  submit  an  assessment  to  the  Council,  qualifying  the  situation,

evaluating its implications for the internal market, listing the alternative measures which

were taken and did not adequately mitigate the situation, and justifying the need to adopt

common  specifications.  Together  with  the  assessment,  the  Commission  shall  also

propose that the Council authorise the adoption of common specifications by means of

a Council implementing act.

The Commission may only start the process of drafting the implementing act referred to

in  the  first  subparagraph  following  prior  authorisation  by  the  Council  by  means  of  the

Council implementing act referred to in the second subparagraph.

The  implementing  acts  referred  to  in  the  first  subparagraph  shall  be  adopted  in

accordance  with  the  examination  procedure  referred  to  in  Article  XX.  Where  the

committee delivers no opinion, the Commission shall not adopt the draft implementing

act and Article 5(4), third subparagraph, of Regulation (EU) No 182/2011 shall apply.

Implementing acts adopted under this paragraph shall be temporary and shall cease to

apply [36 months] after the entry into force of the legislative act repealing or amending

Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012.

3.  Before  preparing  the  draft  of  the  implementing  acts  referred  to  in  paragraph  1  and

paragraph 1a of this Article, the Commission shall inform the committee referred to in

Article  22  of  Regulation  (EU)  No  1025/2012  that  it  considers  that  the  conditions  in,

respectively, paragraph 1 and paragraph 1a of this Article are fulfilled.

4.  When  preparing  the  draft  of  the  implementing  acts  referred  to  in  paragraph  1  and

paragraph 1a, the Commission shall take into account the views of the assigned Expert

Group  as  well  as  of  any  other  relevant  bodies,  and  shall  duly  consult  all  relevant

stakeholders.

5. Where a harmonised standard is adopted by a European standardisation organisation

and proposed to the Commission for the purpose of publishing its reference in the Official

Journal  of  the  European  Union,  the  Commission  shall  assess  that  standard  in

accordance  with  Regulation  (EU)  No  1025/2012.  When  reference  of  a  harmonised

standard is published in the Official Journal of the European Union, the Commission shall

repeal or amend the implementing acts referred to in paragraph 1 or paragraph 1a,  or

parts thereof which cover the same requirements as those covered by that harmonised

standard.

Draft recital

In  order  to  account  for  the  exceptional  nature  of  and  potential  far-reaching

consequences for the functioning of the internal market of a situation in which the lack

of  cooperation  with  international  standardisation  organisations  prevents  European

standardisation  organisations  from  developing  relevant  harmonised  standards,  and

where  such  situation  cannot  be  adequately  mitigated  by  alternative  measures,

implementing powers should exceptionally be conferred on the Council to authorise the

Commission to adopt  common specifications under such circumstances, pursuant to

Article 291(2) of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union.1

1 This draft recital is given as an example and its wording will be refined if appropriate.
