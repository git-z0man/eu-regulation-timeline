# COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING DECISION of 28.11.2025 amending Implementing Decision C(2024)5423 as regards the legal basis and the deadlines for the adoption of the standards (Only the English, French and German texts are authentic) THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION, Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, Having regard to Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 October 2012 on European standardisation, amending Council Directives 89/686/EEC and 93/15/EEC and Directives 94/9/EC, 94/25/EC, 95/16/EC, 97/23/EC, 98/34/EC, 2004/22/EC, 2007/23/EC, 2009/23/EC and 2009/105/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Council Decision 87/95/EEC and Decision No 1673/2006/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council ^(1), and in particular Article 10(1) thereof,

COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING DECISION

of 28.11.2025

amending Implementing Decision C(2024)5423 as regards the legal basis and the deadlines for the adoption of the standards

(Only the English, French and German texts are authentic)

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 October 2012 on European standardisation, amending Council Directives 89/686/EEC and 93/15/EEC and Directives 94/9/EC, 94/25/EC, 95/16/EC, 97/23/EC, 98/34/EC, 2004/22/EC, 2007/23/EC, 2009/23/EC and 2009/105/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Council Decision 87/95/EEC and Decision No 1673/2006/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council ^(1), and in particular Article 10(1) thereof,

Whereas:

## Recitals

<a id="rec-1"></a>
(1) By Implementing Decision C(2024)5423 the Commission requested that CEN, CENELEC and ETSI draft new European standards in support of Union policy on ecodesign requirements for sustainable products and on batteries and waste batteries in support of Article Article 77 and 78 of Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 ^(2). That Regulation establishes requirements on sustainability, safety, labelling, marking and information for the placing on the market or putting into service of batteries, as well as requirements for the collection, treatment, and recycling of waste batteries.

Article 77 of that Regulation provides that certain categories of batteries may only be placed on the market or put into service if they have a product passport (battery passport). Article 78 of Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 specifies the essential requirements for the technical design and operation of that product passport.

<a id="rec-2"></a>
(2) CEN, CENELEC and ETSI have accepted the standardisation request as set out in Implementing Decision C(2024)5423 and the standardisation work is ongoing.

<a id="rec-3"></a>
(3) By adoption of Regulation (EU) 2024/1781 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 June 2024 establishing a framework for the setting of ecodesign requirements for sustainable products, amending Directive (EU) 2020/1828 and Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 and repealing Directive 2009/125/EC it has become necessary to amend the initial standard setting request to include the Ecodesign Regulation as the legal basis for all products.

<a id="rec-4"></a>
(4) The harmonised standard[s] adopted in response to standardisation request set out in Implementing Decision C(2024)5423 as amended by this Decision may be subject to access to documents requests in accordance with Regulation (EC) 1049/2001 of the European Parliament and of the Council. In its judgement of 5 March 2024 (C-588/21 P), the Court recognised that there is an overriding public interest, within the meaning of Article 4(2) of Regulation (EC) 1049/2001, justifying the disclosure of harmonised standards

<a id="rec-5"></a>
(5) In accordance with Article 10(3) of Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012, each standardisation request is subject to acceptance by the relevant ESOs. It is therefore necessary to provide for the rules on validity of this Decision in the event that CEN, CENELEC and ETSI do not accept the amendments to Implementing Decision C(2024)5423 set out in this Decision.

<a id="rec-6"></a>
(6) Implementing Decision C(2024)5423 should therefore be amended accordingly.

<a id="rec-7"></a>
(7) The ESOs, the European stakeholders' organisations receiving Union financing, the Ecodesign Forum, and the Telecommunication Conformity Assessment and Market Surveillance Committee established by Article 45 of Directive 2014/53/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council ^(3) have been consulted.

<a id="rec-8"></a>
(8) The measures provided for in this Decision are in accordance with the opinion of the Committee established by Article 22 of Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

### Article 1

Implementing Decision C(2024)5423 is amended as follows:

- (1) The reference to ‘and on batteries and waste batteries’ in the header of the Decision is deleted

The Regulation introduces a requirement that products may only be placed on the market or put into service if a digital product passport (‘product passport’) is available in accordance with certain requirements and specifies the requirements for the product passport. Articles 9 to 13 specify the essential requirements for the technical design and operation of that product passport.

### Article 2

If CEN or CENELEC or ETSI do not accept the amendments set out in Article 1 of this Decision in accordance with Article 10(3) of Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012, this Decision shall cease to apply and Implementing Decision C(2024)5423 in its version of 31 July 2024 shall continue to apply for the standardisation organisation concerned.
