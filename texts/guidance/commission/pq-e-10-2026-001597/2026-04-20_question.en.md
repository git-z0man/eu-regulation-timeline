Question for written answer E-001597/2026/rev.1

to the Commission

Rule 144

Stefano Cavedagna (ECR), Francesco Torselli (ECR)

Subject: SMEs in the creative manufacturing sector: implementing the AI Act’s support measures concerning skills, sandboxes, templates and procurement

A number of companies and stakeholders in the creative manufacturing industry have highlighted that AI uptake is being hampered by a lack of operational skills across the board, uncertainty about requirements, and difficulties in accessing the AI Act’s measures.

In the EU, AI uptake among large companies (41 % in 2024) is significantly higher than among SMEs (13%).

Articles 57, 62 and 96 and recitals 143 and 145 of the AI Act provide for support measures for SMEs and start-ups which include the provision of priority access to sandboxes, dedicated training and standard templates, as well as compliance cost reductions, the single information platform and best practices for public procurement procedures.

Having regard for the requirements currently in force – which concern AI literacy (Article 4) and other matters – and the upcoming implementing measures:

1. Could the Commission provide clarification as to the timeframe, format and content of the guidelines provided for in Article 96, specifically as regards the SMEs and sectors (including the creative manufacturing and cultural industries) that need the most help with AI?

2. What practical tools (short forms, minimum standards, examples for non-technical profiles) will be made available for manufacturing SMEs that have a strong creative component?

3. What actions will the Commission take in conjunction with the European AI Office and Member States to ensure that micro and small enterprises enjoy genuine access to European Digital Innovation Hubs and that the implementation of the measures referred to in Article 62 is monitored?
