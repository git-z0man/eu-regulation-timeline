EN E-002415/2025 Answer given by Executive Vice-President Virkkunen on behalf of the European Commission (1.8.2025)

The Commission is closely monitoring the situation in Hungary and is assessing the implications that the legislative changes referred to by the Honourable Members may have under EU law, including as regards compliance with the Artificial Intelligence (AI) Act^(1). In this context, the Commission has asked Hungary for certain clarifications as regards the use of facial recognition and is currently analysing the received replies.
