EN P-002257/2026 Answer given by Executive Vice-President Séjourné on behalf of the European Commission (15.7.2026)

The Commission is committed to an open, rules-based and reciprocal trading system. The proposal for an Industrial Accelerator Act (IAA)^(1) is designed to strengthen the EU’s industrial resilience and competitiveness in line with the EU’s international commitments.

Article 8(1) of the IAA provides that with respect to the EU origin requirements referred to in Article 11 of the IAA, content originating in third countries with which the EU has concluded an agreement establishing a free trade area or a customs union, or that are parties to the Agreement on Government Procurement (GPA), where relevant obligations of the EU exist under that agreement, shall be deemed of EU origin. Article 8(2) of the IAA provides for the possibility to exclude, in whole or in part, a third country from the scope of paragraph 1, under certain conditions.

The Commission notes that the application of Article 8 to specific third countries would depend on the status of the relevant agreements and, with respect to the GPA, on the existence of relevant commitments in the area of public procurement, as well as on the application of a possible delegated act under Article 8(2) of the IAA to specific third countries.

The Commission will continue to ensure that the EU’s openness is matched by effective reciprocity and that European industry is not placed at a structural disadvantage where third countries restrict market access.
