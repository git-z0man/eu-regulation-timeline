Priority question for written answer P-003058/2026

to the Commission

Rule 144

Georgiana Teodorescu (ECR)

Subject: Romania’s compliance with its obligation to implement Directive (EU) 2022/2555 (NIS2)

The recent cyberattacks in Romania, which brought critical digital infrastructure such as the National Agency for Cadastre and Land Registration (ANCPI) to a standstill, caused serious disruption to the country’s public services and economic activity.

The NIS2 Directive sets clear rules for Member States on ensuring resilience, designating national authorities in charge of supervision and reporting major incidents. While national authorities are responsible for direct supervision, it is the Commission’s role to monitor the correct application of EU law.

In view of the above, can the Commission answer the following?

1. Were these attacks reported at EU level using the mechanisms provided for in the NIS2 Directive and what technical support has ENISA given to the Romanian authorities so far?

2. Does it consider that Romania has properly transposed and applied the NIS2 supervisory and control framework in the case of critical public services?

3. Do the Commission and ENISA intend to carry out an assessment of the level of cyber resilience of critical digital infrastructure in Romania and to make specific recommendations on strengthening institutional capacity and supervisory mechanisms?
