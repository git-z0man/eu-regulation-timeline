Question for written answer E-003343/2026

to the Commission

Rule 144

Mihai Tudose (S&D)

Subject: The right of EU citizens to give preferential treatment to domestic products

A recent EU-wide opinion poll shows that consumers prefer products from their own countries. This survey comes at a time when the Commission’s proposed Industrial Accelerator Act is putting forward the idea of applying the European preference mechanism to public procurement.

It is a good idea to support European companies given that public procurement accounts for around 17 % of the EU’s economic output.

Member State governments have called for the European preference mechanism to focus on the products themselves, and not on the countries involved.

However, EU citizens have made their feelings known, calling for public funds to prioritise products made in their own countries rather than goods from other Member States.

Is the Commission planning to uphold the right to national preference and, if so, how will it go about this?
