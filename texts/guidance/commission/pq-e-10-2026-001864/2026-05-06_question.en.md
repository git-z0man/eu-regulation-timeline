Question for written answer E-001864/2026

to the Commission

Rule 144

Fabio De Masi (NI)

Subject: Capacity and institutional strengthening of the EU AI Office

The newly established EU AI Office is tasked with monitoring compliance with the Artificial Intelligence Act but has faced criticism for being inadequately equipped^(1).

According to reports, the office currently has no access to advanced models such as Mythos developed by Anthropic, which is reported to potentially outperform human capabilities in detecting software vulnerabilities and could pose risks to critical infrastructure.

While the UK AI Security Institute has already presented a technical analysis, the Commission is not among the organisations that were granted access to the model. Furthermore, the office’s safety unit is reported to be understaffed with around 36 employees and to have a relatively low position within the Commission’s hierarchy.

1. What specific measures is the Commission taking to strengthen the AI Office in terms of staffing, technical expertise and budget, and what measures does it intend to take in the future?

2. Does the Commission intend to upgrade the office’s institutional position to ensure that it can act independently and respond in the event of an acute cybersecurity crisis?

3. What political and diplomatic measures does the Commission intend to take to be able to carry out its own technical analysis of Mythos?

Submitted: 6.5.2026
