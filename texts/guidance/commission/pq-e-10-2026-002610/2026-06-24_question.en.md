Question for written answer E-002610/2026

to the Commission

Rule 144

Markéta Gregorová (Verts/ALE)

Subject: Mandatory disclosure and portability of cloud control servers for connected products on the single market

A growing share of products on the EU market rely on cloud control centres operated through mobile applications, including solar inverters, robotic vacuum cleaners, smart home devices and connected toys. Auditing every such product is neither feasible for Member States nor proportionate for manufacturers, and software updates may alter a product’s behaviour.

A targeted, low-cost measure would address part of this gap. Manufacturers and importers could be required to document all application programming interfaces (APIs) and control server addresses used by a product, including those used by future software versions, in the declaration of conformity and to ensure the control server can be changed by the operator. Such a measure would expose hidden functions, allow control centres to be switched where a manufacturer becomes unreliable, keep products working after insolvency and enable third party integration across brands.

1. Do existing instruments, such as the Cyber Resilience Act or delegated acts under the Radio Equipment Directive, already require disclosure of control server addresses and APIs? If not, will the Commission assess the introduction of such a requirement?

2. Will the Commission examine mandating control server portability to extend product lifespan and reduce lock-in?

3. How will the Commission address the risks posed by software updates that introduce new control endpoints after placement on the market?
