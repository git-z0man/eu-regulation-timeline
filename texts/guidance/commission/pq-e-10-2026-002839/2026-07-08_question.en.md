Question for written answer E-002839/2026

to the Commission

Rule 144

Jessica Polfjärd (PPE)

Subject: Definition of low-carbon steel

Both the Industrial Accelerator Act and the proposed revision of the CO2 standards for cars and vans refer to ‘low-carbon steel’ without defining it. In both cases, the definition is left to future delegated acts under the Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation.

1. Will the Commission ensure a single, coordinated definition of low-carbon steel for both laws, and, if so, by when?

2. How will the Commission avoid the application of two different definitions at the same time?

Submitted: 8.7.2026
