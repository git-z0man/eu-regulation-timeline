Question for written answer E-001071/2026

to the Commission

Rule 144

Bruno Gonçalves (S&D)

Subject: ‘One Europe, One Market’ – clarification regarding Regulation (EU) 2023/988 in order to reduce the fragmentation of textile trade in the EU single market

Diverging interpretations on the applicability to textile products of Articles 9-18 of the General Product Safety Regulation (GPSR)^(1) have raised legal uncertainty and caused fragmentation in the single market.

Article 2 provides that the regulation applies insofar as there are no specific provisions with the same objective under EU harmonisation legislation that governs the safety of such products. The Commission’s guidelines further state that where products are subject to specific safety requirements under EU law, the GPSR applies only to aspects, risks or categories of risk not covered by those requirements.

Textile products are subject to Regulation (EU) No 1007/2011^(2) on textile fibre names and related labelling and marking of the fibre composition of textile products. However, national authorities have interpreted provisions differently regarding Chapter III, Section 1 of the GPSR (Articles 9-18). Some consider that these obligations do not apply to textile products covered by EU harmonisation legislation, while others expect economic operators to comply with such legislation.

In this context:

1. Does the Commission consider that the obligations laid down in Articles 9-18 of Regulation (EU) 2023/988 apply to textile products covered by Regulation (EU) No 1007/2011?

2. If so, which specific obligations under Articles 9-18 apply to such products and what is the legal basis for that interpretation under Article 2 of the GPSR?

Submitted: 16.3.2026
