Question for written answer E-000099/2026

to the Commission

Rule 144

Estrella Galán (The Left), Jaume Asens Llodrà (Verts/ALE), Vicent Marzà Ibáñez (Verts/ALE), Saskia Bricmont (Verts/ALE), Aodhán Ó Ríordáin (S&D), Jonas Sjöstedt (The Left), Hanna Gedin (The Left), Kim Van Sparrentak (Verts/ALE), Mélissa Camara (Verts/ALE), Irene Montero (The Left), Anthony Smith (The Left), Manon Aubry (The Left), Thomas Bajada (S&D), Marit Maij (S&D), Leire Pajín (S&D), Leila Chaibi (The Left), Pierre Jouvet (S&D), Sara Matthieu (Verts/ALE), Elisabeth Grossmann (S&D), Catarina Martins (The Left), Nikos Pappas (The Left), Diana Riba i Giner (Verts/ALE)

Subject: Request for urgent EU measures against AI-enabled non-consensual sexualised content involving minors on platform X

Recent media reports^(1)^(2) indicate that Grok, the artificial intelligence (AI) system integrated into the platform X, has allowed users to generate non-consensual sexualised images and digitally ‘undress’ women and, reportedly, minors, due to failures in its technical safeguards. These incidents pose a serious risk to fundamental rights, in particular the protection of children, and may involve the generation and dissemination of AI-enabled child sexual abuse material.

Given that X is designated as a Very Large Online Platform (VLOP) and that Grok constitutes a generative AI system integrated into the platform, these incidents could indicate breaches of the systemic risk assessment and mitigation obligations laid down in Articles 34 and 35 of the Digital Services Act^(3) and of the safety-by-design requirements established under the Artificial Intelligence Act^(4).

1. Is the Commission currently investigating this case and the extent to which Grok’s failures may constitute breaches of the DSA and the Artificial Intelligence Act?

2. What urgent and concrete measures will the Commission take to ensure that X remedies the deficiencies of the Grok system, removes illegal content, and effectively protects children, adolescents, and particularly women and girls, from abuse enabled by this AI system?

Supporters^(5)

Submitted: 13.1.2026
