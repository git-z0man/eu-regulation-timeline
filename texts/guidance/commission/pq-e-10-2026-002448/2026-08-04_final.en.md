EN E-002448/2026 Answer given by Executive Vice-President Virkkunen on behalf of the European Commission (4.8.2026)

The data centre rating scheme was developed based on the reporting obligations for data centres under the Energy Efficiency Directive^(1). Following the open public consultation on the draft act, the Commission is considering the feedback received from Member States and stakeholders to include the use of nuclear power in the labelling of data centres, in line with the principle of technological neutrality.

Article 4 of the proposed Cloud and AI Development Act^(2) lays down the proposed operational objectives of the Cloud and AI Leadership Initiatives. Article 4(1)(e) of the proposal recognises small modular reactors (SMRs) as one possible energy source for data centres in that context.

Separately, Article 11(1) of the proposal only refers to letters (a) to (n) and does not reference the metrics that concern renewable energy consumption (letters (o) to (r)), which preserves technological neutrality when sustainability requirements are established for data centres in acceleration zones. This approach enables competent authorities to assess projects based on their overall energy performance rather than favouring specific energy generation technologies. Therefore, Article 11 does not penalise data centres powered by SMRs or nuclear in general.
