EN E-005052/2025 Answer given by Executive Vice-President Virkkunen on behalf of the European Commission (23.2.2026)

The Commission is committed to enhancing Europe’s sovereignty and security as well as strengthening its competitiveness. These are key priorities set in the AI Continent Action plan and underpinning the upcoming Cloud and AI Development Act^(1).

Recognising the critical position of cloud technologies in the European digital environment, we are aiming to provide the right regulatory conditions for the development of sovereign cloud and AI computing services in the EU. The Cloud and AI Development Act will foster the use of cloud computing in the EU, in particular for public administrations, and address the issue of having various diverging definitions of sovereign cloud across the EU, which affects the internal market. This will not only uphold Europeans’ rights but also increase Europe’s competitiveness and build its digital infrastructure.

The internal market remains open to all companies regardless of whether they are EU-based or not as long as they comply with EU law, thus respecting the rights of Europeans. For highly critical use cases, including for certain AI applications, sovereignty and operational autonomy require highly secure EU-based cloud capacity. The upcoming Cloud and AI Development Act is expected to ensure that the public and private sector in the EU can rely on such capacity for these use cases, thus laying the basis for the public sector to adopt AI in an environment of trust. More generally, leveraging the existing provisions on cloud switching, the Cloud and AI Development Act will look into fostering the right regulatory conditions for building EU cloud capacity and services.
