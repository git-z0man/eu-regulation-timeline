Question for written answer E-000906/2025

to the Commission

Rule 144

Leila Chaibi (The Left)

Subject: Digital Trade Agreement with Singapore

The recent conclusion of a Digital Trade Agreement between the EU and Singapore, and the imminent conclusion of similar agreements with the Philippines and Thailand, raise concerns about their impact on data protection and the application of the Artificial Intelligence (AI) Act.

These agreements prohibit third countries from requiring access to the source code of EU companies, a measure designed to protect intellectual property. However, this restriction could hamper the work of regulatory authorities, making it more difficult to investigate security breaches and compliance with EU standards. Companies already have legal protections for their business secrets.

1. Why has the Commission introduced binding rules on source code access, which risks hampering the application of the AI Act?

2. Why was a more proportionate approach not adopted, by incorporating this provision into the existing cooperation agreement with Singapore?

3. What is the Commission’s assessment of the compatibility of this provision with the AI Act and can it make that analysis public?

Submitted: 4.3.2025
