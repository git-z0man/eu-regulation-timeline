Question for written answer E-000867/2026

to the Commission

Rule 144

Andi Cristea (S&D)

Subject: Implications of the European Industrial Accelerator Act for the EU-UK strategic economic partnership

The European Industrial Accelerator Act (IAA) is an important piece of legislation that will affect the EU’s relationship with its trading partners, including with the United Kingdom.

To recall:

– the EU and the UK have agreed a strategic partnership;

– the UK is the EU’s third-biggest trading partner (10.1 %);

– the EU’s export of goods to the UK mainly includes machinery and appliances (20.4 %), transport equipment (18.9 %), chemical products (11.5 %), foodstuffs, beverages and tobacco (9.3 %), and base metals (5.5 %).

In the light of the above, could the Commission answer the following questions:

1. Does the Commission agree that the IAA presents the EU with an opportunity to show global leadership and forge stronger economic ties with trusted partners and like-minded economies, such as those that share EU values and an openness to trade and investment?

2. Does the Commission agree that businesses operating across the EU and the UK are the very companies that define a ‘Made with Europe’ approach, as they facilitate trade between the EU and the UK, thus driving growth, competitiveness and security across Europe?
