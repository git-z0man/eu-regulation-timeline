Question for written answer E-003378/2026

to the Commission

Rule 144

Cynthia Ní Mhurchú (Renew)

Subject: Data-driven oversight by national procurement review bodies

Under the EU procurement remedies framework, enforcement of procurement rules in nationally funded procurement largely depends on Member State review bodies. However, many oversight systems remain complaint-led and may not have access to structured data needed to identify systemic risks, recurring breaches or patterns of non-compliance.

1. Does the Commission consider that national procurement review and oversight bodies should have access to structured procurement data to support systemic monitoring, not only individual complaint handling?

2. Will the Commission assess whether the current remedies framework is adequate for modern, data-driven oversight of procurement compliance?

3. In the context of the future public procurement act, will the Commission consider whether national oversight bodies need stronger data access, analytical capacity and reporting obligations?

Submitted: 31.8.2026
