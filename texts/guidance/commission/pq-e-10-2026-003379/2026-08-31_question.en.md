Question for written answer E-003379/2026

to the Commission

Rule 144

Cynthia Ní Mhurchú (Renew)

Subject: Procurement transparency and SME access to public contracts

The Commission has repeatedly emphasised the importance of improving access to public procurement for small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs). However, limited transparency on purchasing patterns, repeat awards, contract aggregation and supplier concentration may make it harder to assess whether procurement markets are genuinely open to smaller firms.

1. Does the Commission consider that better procurement data could help identify barriers to SME’s participation in public contracts?

2. Will the Commission assess whether Member States collect sufficient data on supplier size, repeat awards, contract lots and market concentration to evaluate SME access?

3. In the future Public Procurement Act, will the Commission consider measures to improve transparency on whether public procurement markets remain accessible to SMEs and new entrants?
