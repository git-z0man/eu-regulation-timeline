E-001948/2026

Answer given by Mr McGrath

on behalf of the European Commission

(14.9.2026)

The Commission is aware of Decree-Law No 23/2026 on additional funding for municipal video surveillance systems under the Urban Security Agreements established by Decree-Law No 14/2017.

Processing of personal data remains subject to EU and national law. Where municipalities process personal data, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)^(1) applies, including the principles of lawfulness, necessity, proportionality and the requirements of Articles 24, 25, 32 and, where appropriate, 35. Where competent authorities process data to prevent, investigate, detect, prosecute criminal offences, Directive (EU) 2016/680^(2), as transposed into Italian law, applies. It includes requirements on lawfulness, security, accountability and logging. National supervisory authorities are best placed to assess compliance.

Directive (EU) 2022/2555^(3) requires essential and important entities in 18 critical sectors – including public administrations – to adopt cybersecurity risk management measures and report significant incidents. The Directive excludes entities dealing with national security, public security, defence or law enforcement. Italy notified full transposition by 17 October 2024.

The Commission will continue to monitor the implementation of the relevant EU legislation in this area.
