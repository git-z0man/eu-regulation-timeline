Priority question for written answer P-001672/2026

to the Commission

Rule 144

Piotr Müller (ECR)

Subject: Why is the European Union lagging behind other countries in assessing advanced AI models?

The European Union presents itself as a global leader in AI regulation. However, recent revelations about Anthropic’s advanced AI model called ‘Mythos’ raise serious concerns as to whether the EU has the necessary technical capacity to enforce the rules it has adopted.

While the UK authorities gained access to the model and quickly produced a technical assessment, the Commission reportedly has neither access to the technology nor a sufficient number of experts to assess the cybersecurity risks of cutting-edge AI systems.

This gives the impression that the EU is better at legislating than at understanding or enforcing legislation.

Given this situation:

1. How many technical experts and cybersecurity specialists are currently employed in the departments of the European AI Office responsible for advanced artificial intelligence systems, and how does the Commission assess whether these resources are sufficient?

2. What specific steps is the Commission taking to ensure that the AI Office has access to cutting-edge AI models developed outside the EU and is able to assess them independently?

3. Does the Commission consider that the current structure and resources of the AI Office are sufficient to effectively enforce the Artificial Intelligence Act in the face of rapidly evolving developments in this field?

Submitted: 23.4.2026
