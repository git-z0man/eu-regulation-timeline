Question for written answer E-002079/2026

to the Commission

Rule 144

Juan Ignacio Zoido Álvarez (PPE)

Subject: Huawei equipment purchased in Spain using NextGenerationEU funds

The Spanish press has published documentary evidence showing that the Spanish Government has spent up to EUR 12 million of NextGenerationEU funds to purchase IT equipment from Huawei to store judicial wiretaps.

In recent months, the Commission has repeatedly advised Member States to rid their critical sectors of high-risk suppliers to avoid security gaps in the EU. Most recently, it has done so in its proposed EU Cybersecurity Act. In light of the above:

1. Did the Commission validate the purchase of equipment from a high-risk supplier in Spain’s national recovery and resilience plan?

2. Does the Commission approve of EU funds being used to procure equipment or services from suppliers that pose a high risk to the EU’s cybersecurity?
