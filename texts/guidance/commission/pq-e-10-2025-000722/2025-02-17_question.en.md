Question for written answer E-000722/2025/rev.1

to the Commission

Rule 144

César Luena (S&D)

Subject: Environmental impact of the Commission’s AI Continent strategy

As part of its announcement about the Competitiveness Compass, the Commission launched its AI Continent strategy, which includes the AI Factories initiative, the goal of which is for all the benefits of aggregation and network effects to be leveraged at European level. AI factories are expected to boost Europe’s computing power and make it accessible to start-ups, researchers and industry. Taking into account the Commission’s position in answer E-002228/2024(ASW)^(1) and the increasing amount of fresh water needed to fuel this growing technology:

1. How will the AI Continent strategy be aligned with the goals of the European Green Deal, particularly with regard to water efficiency and emissions reductions?

2. What will the Commission do, within the framework of the Artificial Intelligence Act, to further voluntary application of codes of conduct concerning known or estimated energy consumption and their effectiveness?

Submitted: 17.2.2025
