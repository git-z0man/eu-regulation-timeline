Question for written answer E-002033/2026

to the Commission

Rule 144

Alvise Pérez (NI)

Subject: Use of forensic AI software by the Spanish Tax Agency

According to press reports, the Spanish Tax Agency has acquired licences for Cellebrite Pathfinder Enterprise, a digital forensic analysis tool reportedly used to process information captured from taxpayers’ electronic devices in tax or judicial proceedings.

Experts have warned that the increasing use of artificial intelligence and forensic data-analysis tools by tax authorities may entail serious risks for privacy, proportionality, the presumption of innocence, the right to an effective remedy and the protection of personal data, particularly where large volumes of private information from electronic devices are processed.

In this context:

1. Is the Commission aware of the Spanish Tax Agency’s acquisition or use of Cellebrite Pathfinder Enterprise or similar forensic AI tools?

2. Does the Commission consider that the use of such tools by national tax authorities must be subject to prior authorisation, strict proportionality tests, auditability, data minimisation and effective remedies under EU law?

3. Will the Commission assess whether the use of AI-based forensic tools by national tax authorities complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679), the Charter of Fundamental Rights of the European Union and the AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689)?

Submitted: 18.5.2026
