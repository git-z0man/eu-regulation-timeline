Question for written answer E-002354/2026

to the Commission

Rule 144

Elena Donazzan (ECR), Alessandro Ciriani (ECR), Pietro Fiocchi (ECR), Mariateresa Vivaldini (ECR), Carlo Ciccioli (ECR), Nicola Procaccini (ECR)

Subject: Implementation of Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 on machinery: shortcomings in the technical framework and risks to Europe’s industrial competitiveness

Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 on machinery is due to enter into force on 20 January 2027. With just a few months to go, the Commission is yet to publish its implementation guide and the harmonised standards. This is depriving businesses and duly authorised bodies of the tools necessary to ensure compliance, legal certainty and display the CE marking correctly.

The new requirements, on safety components, artificial intelligence-based systems and digital architectures, necessitate structural overhauls that cannot be completed in the current timeframe in the absence of clear technical guidance.

This affects strategic sectors such as aerospace, defence, energy and advanced engineering and the automotive industry, thus directly jeopardising skilled employment and the Union’s industrial sovereignty.

Can the Comision therefore say:

1. When it plans to publish the application guide and harmonised standards?

2. Whether it intends to adopt transitional measures to enable businesses to comply fully with the new requirements?

3. Whether it has assessed the socio-economic impact of the technical delay on the industrial sectors concerned?

Submitted: 9.6.2026
