Question for written answer E-000632/2026

to the Commission

Rule 144

Anthony Smith (The Left)

Subject: Legal responsibility and the fight against AI-generated racist content

As AI systems – particularly generative models – are being developed, more and more racist, discriminatory and hateful content is being produced or amplified by algorithms. That downward spiral is set against the backdrop of long-standing poor accountability of digital players, which the European Union has sought to redress through the adoption of Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 on digital services and Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 on artificial intelligence.

While those regulations explicitly recognise the risks of bias, discrimination and infringement of fundamental rights, they do not clearly define the liability regime applicable when AI systems generate or disseminate racist content. The question of legal imputability remains central to effectively protecting the people targeted.

In light of the above:

1. How does the Commission intend to ensure effective legal responsibility when AI systems produce or disseminate racist or discriminatory content?

2. What binding obligations will be imposed on companies that design and use AI systems, in order to prevent, identify and correct the discriminatory biases behind such content?

3. How does the Commission ensure that racist content generated by AI systems will be blocked upstream and removed when it is disseminated?
