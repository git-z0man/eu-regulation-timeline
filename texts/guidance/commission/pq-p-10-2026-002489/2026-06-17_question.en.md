Priority question for written answer P-002489/2026

to the Commission

Rule 144

Sunčana Glavak (PPE)

Subject: Digital product passport

The digital product passport is intended to enhance transparency, sustainability and the functioning of the single market. However, many small and medium-sized enterprises are warning that its implementation could result in significant adaptation costs, an additional administrative burden and dependence on private providers of digital solutions and infrastructure.

In light of this:

1. What sector-specific estimate of compliance costs does the Commission foresee for small and medium-sized enterprises, and does it have any figures on the average financial burden that the digital product passport will create for SMEs?

2. Is it planning to establish common European or publicly available digital solutions for the storage, exchange and verification of data, so that companies are not dependent on private IT platforms and commercial service providers?

3. What sources of financial support will be available to SMEs, particularly in less-developed Member States, for investments in the necessary IT systems, digital tools and employee training?
