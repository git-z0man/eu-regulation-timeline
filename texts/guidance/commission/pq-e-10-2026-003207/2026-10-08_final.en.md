EN

E-003207/2026

Answer given by Ms Roswall

on behalf of the European Commission

(8.10.2026)

Ecodesign requirements for textile apparel will be developed with the particularities of Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (SMEs), including micro-enterprises, in mind as they account for the vast majority of businesses active in the European textile apparel value chain. Measures taken to prevent undue negative impacts on businesses can for instance concern the length of transition periods after entry into force of the delegated act, the provision of support tools or targeted exemptions. As set out in the Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation^(1), exemptions should be provided when setting ecodesign requirements where needed, for instance for products produced in very small quantities. Any such remedial measures and their detailed criteria are still under consideration.

As part of the preparation of the delegated act setting out ecodesign requirements for textile apparel products, the Commission will conduct an impact assessment in line with the methodologies set out in the Better Regulation Guidelines^(2). A key element of this assessment is an ‘SME Test’ to ensure effective and efficient rules and minimise the burden imposed on SMEs. Data from an ongoing ecodesign preparatory study will be used for this purpose, as well as additional information collected through consultation of SMEs and their representative organisations. Engagement to seek the opinion of EU SMEs in textile sector will also be fostered via the SME participation in the Ecodesign Forum.
