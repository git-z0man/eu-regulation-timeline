EN

E-001631/2026

Answer given by Ms Roswall

on behalf of the European Commission

(6.7.2026)

The Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation (ESPR)^(1) provisions on unsold products do not restrict the trade in products, nor do they prohibit exporting goods for sale in other markets. Such exports are not considered a circumvention of the prohibition. However, economic operators who export unsold consumer products to third countries solely for destruction must disclose the information required under Article 24 of the ESPR. They can be held liable if they circumvent the destruction prohibition.

Channelling unsold consumer products subject to the prohibition through affiliated or contracted micro and small enterprises for destruction is also considered circumvention. Article 25(2) states that economic operators not subject to the prohibition must not destroy unsold consumer products supplied to them to circumvent it. The national authorities are allowed therefore to take the necessary measures to prevent circumvention via micro and small enterprises if evidence suggests they are being used to bypass the rule, including by the setting up and enforcement of penalties, in accordance with Article 74 ESPR.

National competent authorities are responsible for verifying compliance with the prohibition on destruction. They may use any information, document, finding, statement, or intelligence as evidence in their investigations, following the verification principles in Annex III of the Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/2^(2). Any signals of circumvention from these authorities will be duly considered when determining if an extension of the prohibition to micro and small enterprises is necessary.

## Notes

(1) Regulation (EU) 2024/1781 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 June 2024 establishing a framework for the setting of ecodesign requirements for sustainable products, amending Directive (EU) 2020/1828 and Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 and repealing Directive 2009/125/EC, OJ L, 2024/1781, 28.6.2024.

(2) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/2 of 9 February 2026 laying down rules for the application of Regulation (EU) 2024/1781 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards the details and format for the disclosure of information on discarded unsold consumer products, OJ L, 2026/2, 10.2.2026.
