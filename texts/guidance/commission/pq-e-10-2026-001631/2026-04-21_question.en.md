Question for written answer E-001631/2026

to the Commission

Rule 144

Delara Burkhardt (S&D)

Subject: Preventing circumvention of the ban on the destruction of unsold textiles

Regulation (EU) 2024/1781^(1) (Ecodesign Regulation) prohibits the destruction of unsold textiles by large companies from July 2026 and by medium-sized companies from 2030, while micro and small enterprises are exempt.

In this context:

1. What specific measures are envisaged to address the risk that unsold textiles will be exported to non-EU countries in order to circumvent this prohibition and will subsequently be destroyed there, particularly taking into account EU waste shipment rules?

2. How will the Commission ensure that large companies do not circumvent the prohibition by systematically channelling unsold goods through affiliated or contracted micro and small enterprises?

3. What criteria and data will the Commission use as the basis for assessing whether an extension of the prohibition to micro and small enterprises is necessary because of circumvention practices?

Submitted: 21.4.2026

<a id="notes"></a>
## Notes

(1) Regulation (EU) 2024/1781 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 June 2024 establishing a framework for the setting of ecodesign requirements for sustainable products, amending Directive (EU) 2020/1828 and Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 and repealing Directive 2009/125/EC (OJ L, 2024/1781, 28.6.2024, ELI: http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1781/oj).
