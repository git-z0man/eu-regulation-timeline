EN

E-002551/2026

Answer given by Ms Roswall

on behalf of the European Commission

(25.8.2026)

The Commission recognised the environmental impacts of the shedding of microplastics from synthetic textiles in the EU Strategy for Sustainable and Circular Textiles^(1). In view of the broader environmental impacts of this sector, the Ecodesign for Sustainable Products and Energy Labelling Working Plan 2025-2030^(2) prioritised the preparation of ecodesign requirements for textile apparel. The aim of the requirements is to make products more sustainable and circular by removing the worst performing products from the market, and by driving sustainable consumption through provision of information on product environmental impacts. A preparatory study by the Joint Research Centre is ongoing and aims to identify feasible measures that will be further considered in an impact assessment. This study will include an analysis on the fragmentation of textile into microfibres.

In parallel, the Commission is working on a revision of Environmental Footprint methods^(3) for modelling, calculating, and reporting the life cycle environmental impacts of products, such as textiles. Among other aspects, this revision is expected to integrate methods to address the release of microplastics.
