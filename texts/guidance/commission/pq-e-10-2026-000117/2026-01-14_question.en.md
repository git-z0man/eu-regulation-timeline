Question for written answer E-000117/2026

to the Commission

Rule 144

Tobiasz Bocheński (ECR)

Subject: Insufficient ambition in the Digital Omnibus for Europe’s digital and industrial competitiveness

The Digital Omnibus was presented as a key tool to streamline and modernise the EU’s digital regulatory framework. However, stakeholders across Europe’s digital economy have repeatedly warned that the current proposal lacks the ambition necessary to support innovation, competitiveness and growth, particularly when global technological leadership is increasingly contested.

The digital sector continues to be burdened by fragmented implementation, legal uncertainty and administrative complexity. Rather than accelerating Europe’s ability to innovate and scale up digital solutions, the proposal as it stands offers only some relief to the industry but without addressing existing structural obstacles.

Businesses – especially small and medium-sized enterprises and emerging technology firms – require clear incentives, supportive governance and regulatory flexibility. The Digital Omnibus falls short in providing these elements. It does not sufficiently reduce complexity, nor does it leverage Europe’s industrial strengths or reflect lessons learned from recent digital legislation on how to avoid disproportionate burdens.

1. How does the Commission plan to address the widespread concerns from industry that the Digital Omnibus lacks the ambition required to truly enhance Europe’s digital competitiveness?

2. What concrete steps will the Commission take to ensure that the Digital Omnibus brings positive changes to the AI Act?

3. How will the Commission ensure that future updates to the EU’s digital rulebook prioritise an environment that supports innovation and the growth of European companies in global markets?

Submitted: 14.1.2026
