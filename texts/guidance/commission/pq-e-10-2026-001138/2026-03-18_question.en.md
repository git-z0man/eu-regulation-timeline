Question for written answer E-001138/2026

to the Commission

Rule 144

Dan-Ştefan Motreanu (PPE)

Subject: AI Act

Several European industry associations representing the digital and telecommunications sectors have raised concerns about the implementation of the AI Act, in particular regarding possible regulatory overlaps with existing EU product safety legislation and the cumulative impact of multiple digital regulations on businesses. Stakeholders contend that some sectors may be subject to double regulation, notably where AI systems are already covered by sector-specific safety frameworks.

Furthermore, industry representatives have emphasised the need for more alignment between the implementation timetable of the AI Act, the digital simplification initiatives currently under discussion, and the broader EU data protection framework, particularly with regard to the rules governing the use of data for the development and deployment of AI systems.

Finally, calls have been made to consider postponing certain upcoming application deadlines of the AI Act in order to provide greater legal certainty and allow companies sufficient time to comply.

Therefore, what approach is the Commission considering to address possible overlap and legal uncertainty arising from the AI Act, and could revisions to its scope or implementation timeline be envisaged to maintain consistency within the EU legal framework and to promote competitiveness?
