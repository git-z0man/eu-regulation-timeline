Question for written answer E-003771/2025

to the Commission

Rule 144

Wouter Beke (PPE)

Subject: Application of the NIS2 Directive

Recent concerns from the Belgian education sector highlight practical difficulties in the implementation of the NIS2 Directive^(2)1. Large school institutions with extensive solar panel installations are formally classified as ‘producers’ of electricity and thus fall within the scope of the directive. However, such entities are not the highly critical operators that the NIS2 Directive was originally intended to cover. The directive’s current simplification provisions are insufficient to address these disproportionate obligations, as these schools remain within the scope of the directive, which imposes significant costs on institutions that often lack in-house cybersecurity expertise.

In the light of the above:

1. Is the Commission prepared to take additional simplification or clarification measures to reduce the disproportionate burdens imposed by the NIS2 Directive on institutions that are not classified among the critical sectors and operators it was originally intended to cover?

2. How does the Commission assess the possibility of simplifying the scope of the directive, for example by introducing clear thresholds, such as 1 megawatt capacity, or exemptions for entities that mainly generate electricity for self-consumption?

Submitted: 29.9.2025
