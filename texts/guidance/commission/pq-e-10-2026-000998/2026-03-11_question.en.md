Question for written answer E-000998/2026

to the Commission

Rule 144

Lara Magoni (ECR)

Subject: Fraud in the cultural and creative sectors through misuse of artificial intelligence

Fraud associated with the cultural and creative content generated by artificial intelligence is a systemic challenge to the entire European creative sector, including music, the visual arts, graphic design, audiovisual works, digital publishing and podcasts.

Bots, automated traffic and manipulative practices can be used to generate large-scale artificial listening, viewing and interaction, hence altering algorithms, rankings and remuneration systems. These practices deprive legitimate creators of revenue and visibility, diminish content pluralism and undermine consumer trust, leading to a distorted market.

For example, in the music sector, around 10 % of the new tracks uploaded each day are generated by AI, of which 85 % are fraudulent, which goes to show the scale of the phenomenon. Such practices violate Article 50 of the AI Act, which states that AI-generated content must be clearly identifiable to ensure transparency, fairness and copyright protection.

In the light of this damage to a key sector of Europe’s economy and identity, can the Commission answer the following questions:

1. How will it ensure the transparent and uniform implementation of the obligations arising from the AI Act in the digital creative sector?

2. What measures is it considering to ensure economic protection, visibility and recognition for bona fide creators and to strengthen consumer trust in the EU digital market?
