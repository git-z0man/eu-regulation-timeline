EN

E-002355/2026

Answer given by Ms Roswall

on behalf of the European Commission

(25.8.2026)

Bio-based materials and products are at the core of the Strategic framework for a competitive and sustainable EU bioeconomy^(1). The Commission is implementing this framework through different initiatives, including the upcoming Biotech Act II^(2), the Circular Economy Act (CEA)^(3), and dedicated work on bio-based plastics under the Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation^(4). These initiatives will support the uptake of bio-based materials where they offer the best environmental and economic performance, and will also improve the collection, treatment and use of biowaste.

Ensuring that products placed on the EU market become more sustainable over their whole life cycle is a core objective of the Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation (ESPR)^(5). The Commission will carry out an impact assessment for each priority product^(6) and propose the most effective and proportionate set of measures for the ESPR 2025-2030 work programme. Aspects concerning sustainable sourcing and circularity could be included where relevant. A technology- and material-neutral approach will ensure recognising the benefits and impacts of different materials, including bio-based materials.

In parallel, environmental footprint (EF) methods^(7) are being revised to acknowledge the differences between biobased and fossil materials, such as carbon uptake^(8).
