Question for written answer E-002355/2026

to the Commission

Rule 144

Katri Kulmuni (Renew)

Subject: Recognising renewable bio-based materials as circular solutions

Based on the available information regarding the Commission’s upcoming proposal for a circular economy act, the act will focus heavily on secondary raw materials and less on bio-based materials. Renewable and bio-based materials should qualify as circular materials in the same way as recycled materials when developing measures to boost demand for circular materials and products. This would also provide an important link between the circular economy act and the EU’s bioeconomy strategy.

I see a risk that the two main initiatives are being developed in silos, where the circular economy act focuses only on waste and secondary raw materials and the bioeconomy strategy, including the forthcoming biotech act II, focuses on bio-based inputs.

With the above in mind:

1. How will the Commission take renewable bio-based materials and products into account in the upcoming EU initiatives, especially in the circular economy act?

2. How will the Commission ensure that renewability and renewable bio-based materials are specifically considered in the Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation^(1)?

3. How is the Commission ensuring that the Product Environmental Footprint methodology development is aligned with the bioeconomy strategy, including the crediting of carbon removal and storage for bio-based materials and products containing biogenic carbon, and in contrast to fossil-based alternatives?

Submitted: 9.6.2026

## Notes

(1) 1 Regulation (EU) 2024/1781 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 June 2024 establishing a framework for the setting of ecodesign requirements for sustainable products, amending Directive (EU) 2020/1828 and Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 and repealing Directive 2009/125/EC (OJ L, 2024/1781, 28.6.2024, ELI: http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1781/oj).
