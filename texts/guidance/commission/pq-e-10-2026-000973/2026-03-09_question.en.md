Question for written answer E-000973/2026

to the Commission

Rule 144

Juan Ignacio Zoido Álvarez (PPE)

Subject: Cybersecurity breach in Spain

On 28 February 2026, several media outlets reported that the Spanish National Police and Civil Guard systems had been hacked, with data linked to credentials of agents from both entities being stolen. The next day, it emerged that the same cybercriminals had also accessed the personal information of members of Spain’s National Security Council, including the President of the Government, several ministers and the Chief of Defence Staff.

These events occurred against a backdrop of a sharp increase of cyberattacks in Spain – cyberattacks have increased by 26 % over the past year, with over 122 000 incidents in 2025. Despite this, Spain has still not transposed the NIS 2 Directive, which aims to reinforce the cybersecurity and resilience of the EU’s critical infrastructure.

In light of the above:

1. In the Commission's view, do these kinds of breaches pose a threat to EU security?

2. What support measures does the Commission envisage to make Member States more resilient to these kinds of attacks?

3. Does the Commission believe that Spain’s failure to implement the NIS 2 Directive leaves the country and its companies in a vulnerable position and that the Spanish Government should enforce this transposition?
