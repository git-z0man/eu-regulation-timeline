Question for written answer E-001101/2026

to the Commission

Rule 144

Fulvio Martusciello (PPE)

Subject: Industrial Accelerator Act and the risk of regulatory complexity for EU industry

The Commission has brought forward a proposal for an Industrial Accelerator Act to strengthen Europe’s industrial capacity and accelerate decarbonisation in strategic sectors.

The proposal includes measures such as European origin criteria in public procurement, specific conditions for certain investments and new authorisation procedures for industrial projects.

This initiative is part of the Union’s recent industrial policies, alongside the Net-Zero Industry Act, the Critical Raw Materials Act and the objectives under the Clean Industrial Deal.

Over the past two decades, the EU’s share of global manufacturing production has fallen from 25 % to less than 15 %, with many companies reporting that regulatory complexity and authorisation times are among the main barriers to industrial investment.

Europe cannot afford complexity cloaked as ambition, especially when the aim is to strengthen industrial competitiveness and the functioning of the European single market.

In the light of the above, can the Commission answer the following questions:

1. Does it think that the measures under the proposed regulation are properly weighted against the risk of new regulatory complexity?

2. Have measures been envisaged to ensure realistic implementation of the regulation in the Member States?

3. How does it intend to define and apply the European preference in public procurement provided for in the proposal for a regulation?
