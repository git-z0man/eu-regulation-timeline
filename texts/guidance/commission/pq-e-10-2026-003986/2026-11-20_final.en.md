EN E-003986/2026 Answer given by Executive Vice-President Séjourné on behalf of the European Commission (20.11.2026)

The proposal for the Public Procurement Act^(1) (PPA) creates an enabling framework for public buyers to incorporate into procurement procedures environmental and social considerations, including those related to the quality and sustainability of food. The Commission is of the view that the enabling framework as proposed in Article 53 on food procurement is most appropriate to steer food procurement towards a more quality-oriented approach, while preserving the necessary flexibility for public buyers to adapt the requirements to the specificities of each individual procurement.

The PPA was proposed by the Commission on 9 September 2026 and is currently under discussion by the co-legislators. The question of possible guidelines for the implementation of the future new rules will only arise after adoption of a final text by the EU legislator.
