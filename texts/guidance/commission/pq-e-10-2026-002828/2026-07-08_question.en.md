Question for written answer E-002828/2026

to the Commission

Rule 144

Şerban Dimitrie Sturdza (ECR)

Subject: Protecting minors from generative artificial intelligence systems in the context of the digital omnibus measures simplifying the AI Act

The Commission proposed the digital omnibus with the aim of simplifying some obligations under Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 on artificial intelligence and thus reducing the administrative burden on European businesses and strengthening competitiveness.

Generative AI systems are accessible to minors and can create or recommend text, images, audio and videos. These systems pose risks to children, including exposure to unsuitable content, behavioural manipulation, the creation of harmful content and effects on mental development. The protection of children in the digital environment is guaranteed by Articles 24 and 14(3) of the EU Charter of Fundamental Rights, which enshrine the right of the child to protection and the right of parents to ensure that children are educated according to their convictions.

1. How will the Commission ensure that the simplification of the AI Act through the digital omnibus does not lower the level of protection of minors from generative artificial intelligence systems, in particular with regard to exposure to age-inappropriate content?

2. What concrete support is the Commission offering to allow parents to effectively exercise their right to control and consent to the exposure of their young children to these types of systems?

3. How will consistency be ensured between the simplification measures and the obligations already provided for in the Digital Services Act with regard to the protection of minors, so that no regulatory gaps emerge?
