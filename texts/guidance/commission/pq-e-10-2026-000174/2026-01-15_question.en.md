Question for written answer E-000174/2026

to the Commission

Rule 144

Miriam Lexmann (PPE), Matej Tonin (PPE), Loránt Vincze (PPE), Davor Ivo Stier (PPE), Christophe Gomart (PPE), Paulius Saudargas (PPE)

Subject: Investigation of AI tools providing ‘undressing applications’

Recent reports have revealed the widespread availability of so-called ‘undressing applications.’ These AI-powered tools allow users to digitally remove clothing from people in photographs. Critically, these tools do not distinguish between adults and children, making them capable of producing child sexual abuse material.

The revision of the Directive on combating the sexual abuse and sexual exploitation of children and child pornography^(2)1 already recognises the severity of AI generated material. In addition, nude or otherwise sexually explicit materials produced and shared without consent of the persons, even if they are an adult, gravely damage dignity and breaches the right to privacy and personality. As such, it can be a source of bullying, blackmailing or revenge. AI that is not in compliance with the ethics-by-default principle is also breaching the EU Artificial Intelligence Act^(3)2, which establishes ethics and trustworthiness as its horizontal principles.

In the light of this information, we would like to ask the Commission:

1. Does it intend to investigate companies providing ‘undressing’ features within their AI driven tools under the existing legislation?

2. Does it plan to present an initiative addressing this harmful trend?

Submitted: 15.1.2026
