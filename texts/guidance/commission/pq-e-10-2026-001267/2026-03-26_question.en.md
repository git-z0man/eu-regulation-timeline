Question for written answer E-001267/2026

to the Commission

Rule 144

Joachim Streit (Renew)

Subject: Lethal autonomous weapon systems and use of artificial intelligence in defence

The Commission is promoting the use of artificial intelligence (AI) in defence, in particular under the European Defence Fund and in the ‘White Paper for European Defence – Readiness 2030’. At the same time, military applications are expressly excluded from the scope of the AI Act. Indeed, Article 7 of the act requires that supported measures be compatible with international law and ethical principles, while Article 10 contains no specific provisions on AI or autonomous weapon systems. Meanwhile, Parliament expressly called for a ban on lethal autonomous weapon systems in its resolution of 20 January 2021 and stressed the need for rigorous human oversight.

1. How does Commission explain the fact that military applications are excluded from the scope of the AI Act at the same time as AI-based defence technologies are being supported by the European Defence Fund?

2. How are Parliament’s calls for restrictions or even a ban on autonomous weapon systems being taken into account in AI advancements in defence?

3. What requirements are in place for EU-funded defence projects to ensure clear responsibilities, sufficient human oversight, and transparency over the allocation of EU funds, and is the Commission planning further legislation or will the existing regulatory gaps remain?

Submitted: 26.3.2026
