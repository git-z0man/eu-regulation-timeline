Question for written answer E-003567/2026

to the Commission

Rule 144

Morten Løkkegaard (Renew)

Subject: The forthcoming European Product Act

1. How will the Commission ensure that every product sold to EU consumers through an online platform has a verified and genuinely accountable economic operator established in the EU?

2. Where no accountable EU-based operator or seller can be traced by authorities, will the Commission ensure that the online platform facilitating the sale assumes responsibility for compliance with applicable EU product rules in order to protect children and businesses in the EU?

Submitted: 9.9.2026
