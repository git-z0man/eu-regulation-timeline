Question for written answer E-004566/2025

to the Commission

Rule 144

Christine Anderson (ESN)

Subject: Digital Omnibus 2: safeguards against function creep in EU digital infrastructure if GDPR rules are relaxed

Several ongoing EU initiatives – including the European digital identity wallet, digital travel credentials, border databases, and platform obligations under the Digital Services Act^(2)1 – increasingly rely on cross-system interoperability. If the Digital Omnibus weakens the constraints placed on secondary use or data repurposing by the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)^(3)2, these interconnected systems may allow cross-database profiling or behavioural inference that current legislation was not designed to permit.

1. What concrete safeguards will the Commission introduce to prevent cross-system profiling or inference between identity, travel, communication or platform datasets if GDPR restrictions on secondary processing are relaxed?

2. Will the Commission commit to a cumulative fundamental rights assessment explaining how GDPR relaxation affects the entire digital governance architecture, not only AI-specific use cases?

3. How will the Commission ensure that interoperability initiatives cannot be used to justify broader data flows under a more permissive interpretation of necessity, proportionality or public-interest grounds?

Submitted: 17.11.2025
