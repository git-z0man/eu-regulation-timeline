Question for written answer E-001210/2026/rev.1

to the Commission

Rule 144

Michael McNamara (Renew)

Subject: Compliance costs of the AI Act

The cost of compliance with the AI Act^(1) is estimated to be between EUR 320,000 and EUR 600,000, for entities under the high-risk classification^(2).

1. What compliance costs were estimated by the 2021 study, requested by the Commission, to support the impact assessment of the regulatory requirements prior to the AI Act?

2. Does the Commission or European AI Office have a more recent or authoritative estimate of compliance costs regarding the high-risk provisions?

Submitted: 24.3.2026

<a id="notes"></a>
## Notes

(1) Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 June 2024 laying down harmonised rules on artificial intelligence and amending Regulations (EC) No 300/2008, (EU) No 167/2013, (EU) No 168/2013, (EU) 2018/858, (EU) 2018/1139 and (EU) 2019/2144 and Directives 2014/90/EU, (EU) 2016/797 and (EU) 2020/1828 (Artificial Intelligence Act) (OJ L, 2024/1689, 12.7.2024, ELI: http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj).

(2) https://www.theparliamentmagazine.eu/partner/article/why-the-ai-act-could-cost-billions-to-europes-innovators
