# Management Board Decision MB/2025/13 setting up the ENISA Advisory Group

MANAGEMENT BOARD DECISION

DECISION No MB/2025/13

OF THE MANAGEMENT BOARD OF THE EUROPEAN UNION AGENCY FOR

CYBERSECURITY (ENISA)

of 11 July 2025,

setting up the ENISA Advisory Group

THE MANAGEMENT BOARD OF ENISA

Having regard to

- • Regulation (EU) 2019/881 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 April 2019 on ENISA (the European Union Agency for Cybersecurity) and on information and communications technology cybersecurity certification and repealing Regulation (EU) No 526/2013 (Cybersecurity Act) ^(1), in particular Article 21(1) and (2) thereof;

- • Decision No MB/2020/02 of the Management Board of ENISA of 3 February 2020 on the Establishment and Operation of the Advisory Group, in particular Articles 1, 2 and 7 thereof.

Whereas

## Recitals

<a id="rec-1"></a>
(1) The Advisory Group is a body created pursuant to Article 21 of the Cybersecurity Act;

<a id="rec-2"></a>
(2) It is recommended that a multi-disciplinary group of experts be set up in order to advise ENISA in respect of the performance of its tasks, except of the provisions of Title III of the Cybersecurity Act, and in particular, advise the Executive Director on the drawing up of a proposal for ENISA’s annual work programme, and ensure communication with the relevant stakeholders on issues related to the annual work programme;

<a id="rec-3"></a>
(3) The Management Board, acting on a proposal from the Executive Director, shall establish in a transparent manner the ENISA Advisory Group which should be composed of:

a. recognised experts representing the relevant stakeholders, such as the ICT industry, providers of electronic communications networks or services available to the public, SMEs, operators of essential services, consumer groups, academic experts in the field of cybersecurity, hereinafter referred to as representatives selected ‘ad personam’ b. nominated representatives of competent authorities notified in accordance with Directive (EU) 2018/1972, of European standardisation organisations, as well as of law enforcement and data protection supervisory authorities;

<a id="rec-4"></a>
(4) The Executive Director may invite observers to attend the Advisory Group meetings. Representatives of other bodies deemed to be relevant by the Executive Director, who are not members of the ENISA Advisory Group, may be invited to attend the meetings of the ENISA Advisory Group and to participate in its work.

Article 3

The term of office of the Advisory Group shall run for two-and-a-half years, from 1 August 2025 to 31 January 2028.

Article 4

1. Where it is necessary to replace an ‘ad personam’ appointee of the Advisory Group, the Management Board, acting on a proposal of the Executive Director, may appoint a replacement from the reserve list under Article 1(2), in accordance with Article 6 of Decision No MB/2020/2.. The selected person shall hold the position as ‘ad personam’ member in the Advisory Group for the remainder of its term of establishment.

2. The mandate as ‘ad personam’ member shall be annulled, if an ‘ad personam’ member fails to take part in two consecutive meetings or if an ‘ad personam’ member fails to respond to a single written validation request regarding outputs of the ENISA Single Programming Document within 9 months.

3. The members of the Advisory Group shall notify the ENISA Advisory Group secretariat in case of any change of their affiliation during the mandate.

Done by written procedure on 11 July 2025.
