Aﬀected EU Member States are aware of the situation and are currently investigating the incidents, while providing guidance.

The EU CSIRTs Network (EU Member States-appointed CSIRTs and CERT-EU) and EU-CyCLONe (EU national authorities in charge of cyber crisis) are actively exchanging information and monitoring the situation. ENISA and all relevant EU stakeholders will continue to monitor the incidents and contribute to the overall situational awareness at Union level.

The latest advisories published by CSIRTs Network members can be found on their relevant oﬃcial communication channels. ENISA maintains an advisory collection under: CNW/advisories/2026/CNW-2026-05_retail-incidents.md at main · enisaeu/CNW · GitHub EU Policy

Organisations should be aware that as of 11 September 2026, manufacturers of hardware and software products must report severe incidents and actively exploited vulnerabilities through the Cyber Resilience Act’s Single Reporting platform, in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 (Cyber Resilience Act). More information is available at: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cra-reporting and www.enisa.europa.eu/cra-srp.

The main cybersecurity requirements laid down in the Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) will apply as of 11 December 2027. Manufacturers of hardware and software products (‘products with digital elements’) will be required to implement securityby-design and ensure security support during the lifecycle of such products, including the remediation of vulnerabilities without delay.

In addition, entities to which the NIS2 Directive (Directive (EU) 2022/2555) applies must implement measures to manage the cybersecurity risks to their operations, and report signiﬁcant incidents to their national CSIRT or authority. Furthermore, ENISA is actively contributing to strengthening cybersecurity across the EU by promoting secure-by-design and secure-bydefault principles in the EU market. Among its key initiatives are the publication of the Secure by Design and Default Playbook and regular Technical Advisories on product security. ENISA also supports information sharing and cooperation as mandated by the NIS2 Directive.
