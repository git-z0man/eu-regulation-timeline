# COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING REGULATION (EU) …/... of XXX laying down templates for the application of Regulation (EU) 2025/327 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards procedures supporting access to electronic health data for secondary use

COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING REGULATION (EU) …/...

of XXX

laying down templates for the application of Regulation (EU) 2025/327 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards procedures supporting access to electronic health data for secondary use

(Text with EEA relevance)

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2025/327 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 February 2025 on the European Health Data Space and amending Directive 2011/24/EU and Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 ^(1), and in particular Article 70 thereof,

Whereas:

## Recitals

<a id="rec-1"></a>
(1) Regulation (EU) 2025/327 establishes a framework for the secondary use of electronic health data within the European Health Data Space. It lays down procedures for submitting health data access applications, issuing data permits and submitting health data requests to obtain responses in an anonymised statistical format without access to the underlying electronic health data.

<a id="rec-2"></a>
(2) Common templates are necessary to present the information required for the procedures laid down in Articles 67, 68 and 69 of Regulation (EU) 2025/327 in a clear, structured and uniform manner. Those templates give effect to the requirements of that Regulation without introducing additional conditions for granting access. To reduce administrative burden, they distinguish information required in all cases from conditional and optional information and enable the reuse of information available in dataset catalogues or earlier submissions, provided that it remains accurate, applicable and accessible to the body assessing the application or request.

<a id="rec-3"></a>
(3) The templates should assist health data access bodies, including the Commission acting as the Union health data access service in accordance with Article 56 of Regulation (EU) 2025/327, and, where applicable, trusted health data holders in examining health data access applications and health data requests. The application template should also support the handling of applications submitted under Article 67(3) of that Regulation to authorised participants in HealthData@EU. The data permit template should support those health data access bodies in issuing and amending data permits under Article 68 of that Regulation.

<a id="rec-4"></a>
(4) The common structure and content of the templates should facilitate cooperation between health data access bodies, the Union health data access service and, where applicable, trusted data holders and authorised participants in HealthData@EU, including in cross-border cases. The information collected through the templates should also facilitate reporting by health data access bodies and the Union health data access service, under Article 59 of Regulation (EU) 2025/327.

<a id="rec-5"></a>
(5) To facilitate their use, the templates should be made publicly available in reusable electronic formats on the Commission’s website. Their implementation in the digital systems used by health data access bodies and the Union health data access service, and in the relevant services of HealthData@EU, should preserve the information items and instructions set out in the Annexes to this Regulation. Exchanges of best practices within the EHDS Board pursuant to Article 94(2) of Regulation (EU) 2025/327 may support the consistent use of the templates, including through nonbinding practical guidance and examples.

<a id="rec-6"></a>
(6) The European Data Protection Supervisor was consulted in accordance with Article 42(1) of Regulation (EU) 2018/1725 of the European Parliament and of the Council ^(2) and delivered an opinion on xxxxx

<a id="rec-7"></a>
(7) The measures provided for in this Regulation are in accordance with the opinion of the committee referred to in Article 98(1) of Regulation (EU) 2025/327,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

<a id="art-1"></a>
### Article 1

1. Health data applicants shall use the template set out in Annex I to submit health data access applications referred to in Article 67 of Regulation (EU) 2025/327. They shall also use that template for requests to amend a data permit pursuant to Article 68(13) of that Regulation, or an access approval issued by an authorised participant in HealthData@EU.

2. Health data access bodies, including the Commission acting as the Union health data access service, shall use the template set out in Annex II when issuing or amending data permits referred to in Article 68 of Regulation (EU) 2025/327.

3. Health data applicants shall use the template set out in Annex III to submit health data requests referred to in Article 69 of Regulation (EU) 2025/327.

4. The templates referred to in paragraphs 1, 2 and 3 shall be completed in accordance with the instructions contained in the respective Annexes, including those concerning conditional and optional information.

<a id="art-2"></a>
### Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.

It shall apply from 26 March 2029.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels,

For the Commission The President Ursula VON DER LEYEN

## Annex I – Template for a health data access application

This template is to be used to submit a health data access application as referred to in Article 67 of Regulation (EU) 2025/327 of the European Parliament and of the Council ^(1). General instructions to the applicant (1) Complete each item unless it is marked ‘Optional’ or is subject to a condition that does not apply to your application. Where a condition applies, provide the information requested under that item. Do not repeat information already provided elsewhere in this application; refer to the relevant point instead.

- (2) Where an item concerns more than one natural or legal person, dataset, activity or document, provide a separate set of the requested information for each, unless that item allows information to be grouped.

- (3) Your health data access application will be made public through electronic means without undue delay after initial reception, in accordance with Article 57(1), first subparagraph, point (j)(ii), of Regulation (EU) 2025/327. Complete the application on the basis that its content will be public.

Do not include personal electronic health data or personal data that this Annex does not expressly request. Use professional or official addresses and contact details wherever possible. Email addresses will be included in the public version. Telephone numbers and private residential addresses will not be included. If you consider that publication of specific information would be contrary to applicable Union or national law, complete optional point 7.2. You must still provide the complete information to the competent health data access body or authorised participant. The health data access body or authorised participant will decide whether and to what extent the information concerned is withheld from the public version.

- (4) Where the application concerns electronic health data from an authorised participant in HealthData@EU referred to in Article 75(4) or (5) of Regulation (EU) 2025/327, references to the body deciding on access are to be understood as referring to that authorised participant, where relevant. References in this template to a decision granting or amending access refer, where relevant, to the authorised participant’s access approval or its amendment.

- (5) For data held by Union institutions, bodies, offices or agencies, the Commission performs the functions of the health data access body through the Union health data access service, in accordance with Article 56 of Regulation (EU) 2025/327. References to a health data access body in this template also cover the Commission in that role.

- (6) You may reuse information or supporting documents from an earlier application, provided that you check that they remain accurate and applicable. A reference to an earlier submission may replace resubmission only where the information concerned is accessible to the body assessing the current application and is clearly identified.
