# COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING DECISION of 2.10.2020 on a standardisation request to the European Telecommunications Standards Institute as regards hand-held mobile phones in support of Directive 2014/53/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council in conjunction with Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/320 (Only the English, French and German texts are authentic) THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION, Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, Having regard to Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 October 2012 on European standardisation, amending Council Directives 89/686/EEC and 93/15/EEC and Directives 94/9/EC, 94/25/EC, 95/16/EC, 97/23/EC, 98/34/EC, 2004/22/EC, 2007/23/EC, 2009/23/EC and 2009/105/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Council Decision 87/95/EEC and Decision No 1673/2006/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council, ^(1) and in particular Article 10(1) thereof,

COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING DECISION

of 2.10.2020

on a standardisation request to the European Telecommunications Standards Institute as regards hand-held mobile phones in support of Directive 2014/53/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council in conjunction with Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/320

(Only the English, French and German texts are authentic)

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 October 2012 on European standardisation, amending Council Directives 89/686/EEC and 93/15/EEC and Directives 94/9/EC, 94/25/EC, 95/16/EC, 97/23/EC, 98/34/EC, 2004/22/EC, 2007/23/EC, 2009/23/EC and 2009/105/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Council Decision 87/95/EEC and Decision No 1673/2006/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council, ^(1) and in particular Article 10(1) thereof,

Whereas:

## Recitals

<a id="rec-1"></a>
(1) Directive 2014/53/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council ^(2) establishes a regulatory framework for the making available on the market and putting into service in the Union of radio equipment. The essential requirements for such equipment are laid down in Article 3(3) of that Directive.

<a id="rec-2"></a>
(2) Pursuant to Article 3(3) of Directive 2015/53/EU, the Commission is to specify which categories or classes of radio equipment are concerned by each of the essential requirements set out in points (a) to (i) of the first subparagraph of Article 3(3) of that Directive.

<a id="rec-3"></a>
(3) Point (g) of Article 3(3) of Directive 2014/53/EU provides that the radio equipment is to support certain features ensuring access to emergency services.

<a id="rec-4"></a>
(4) In Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/320 ^(3), the Commission has specified that the essential requirements set out in Article 3(3)(g) of Directive 2014/53/EU are to apply to hand-held mobile telephones with features similar to those of a computer in terms of capability to treat and store data (‘smartphones’). It is also specified that compliance with those requirements is to be ensured through technical solutions for the reception and processing of Wi-Fi data, data from Global Navigation Satellite Systems compatible and interoperable with at least the Galileo system referred to in Regulation (EU) No 1285/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council ^(4), and for the making available of that data for transmission in emergency communications. Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/320 is to apply from 17 March 2022.

<a id="rec-5"></a>
(5) In accordance with Article 16 of Directive 2014/53/EU, radio equipment which is in conformity with harmonised standards or parts thereof the references of which have been published in the Official Journal of the European Union is to be presumed to be in conformity with the essential requirements of that Directive covered by those standards or parts thereof.

<a id="rec-6"></a>
(6) Harmonised standards for radio equipment should help to provide access to emergency services by ensuring that the equipment is designed in such a way that it supports the features required for such access and thus contribute to the free movement of radio equipment, in particular smartphones, in the Union. Given that such standards are technology-neutral and performance-based, they also contribute to ensuring equal conditions of competition among relevant economic operators manufacturing radio equipment, in particular small and medium-sized enterprises.

<a id="rec-7"></a>
(7) The satellite navigation system established under the Galileo programme on the basis of Regulation (EU) No 1285/2013 is a global navigation satellite system ('GNSS') developed by the Union and specifically designed for civilian purposes. It can be used by a variety of public and private actors in Europe and worldwide. The Galileo system can be used in combination with other GNSS.

<a id="rec-8"></a>
(8) Action in support of the Space Strategy for Europe ^(5) is one of the strategic priorities for European standardisation in support of Union legislation and policies, as stated in point 2.6 of the Annual Union Work Programme for European standardisation for 2019 ^(6).

<a id="rec-9"></a>
(9) The European Telecommunications Standards Institute (ETSI) has indicated that the work covered by the standardisation request falls within its area of competence.

<a id="rec-10"></a>
(10) It is therefore appropriate to request that ETSI draft new harmonised standards in support of Article 3(3)(g) of Directive 2014/53/EU in conjunction with Article 1 of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/320.

<a id="rec-11"></a>
(11) The harmonised standards should include detailed technical specifications of the requirements set out in Article 3(3)(g) of Directive 2014/53/EU in conjunction with Article 1 of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/320, especially with respect to the design and manufacture of smartphones. They should also indicate clearly the correlation between the technical specifications and the requirements they aim to cover. They should be based on risk assessment and risk reduction methodologies and reflect the generally acknowledged state of the art.

<a id="rec-12"></a>
(12) Information as to which requirements are covered by a harmonised standard is necessary when assessing, in accordance with Article 10(5) of Regulation (EU) 1025/2012, the compliance of the documents drafted by ETSI. Such information is also necessary before publication of the references of the harmonised standards in the Official Journal of the European Union in accordance with Article 10(6) of Regulation (EU) 1025/2012. In each harmonised standard, ETSI should therefore describe the extent to which it aims to cover the requirement set out in Article 3(3)(g) of Directive 2014/53/EU in conjunction with Article 1 of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/320.

<a id="rec-13"></a>
(13) The European Standardisation Organisations (ESOs) have agreed to follow the Guidelines for the execution of standardisation requests ^(7).

<a id="rec-14"></a>
(14) In order to ensure transparency and facilitate the execution of the requested standardisation activities, ETSI should prepare a work programme and submit it to the Commission.

<a id="rec-15"></a>
(15) In order to enable the Commission to better monitor the requested standardisation work, ETSI should provide the Commission with access to an overall project plan containing detailed information on the execution of the standardisation request and with annual reports on the progress made in the execution of the request and the implementation of the work programme.

<a id="rec-16"></a>
(16) Experience shows that during execution of the standardisation request, it may be necessary to adjust the scope of the request or the deadlines set therein. ETSI should therefore promptly report to the Commission if it considers that more time is required to draft the standards than initially foreseen or that it is appropriate to adapt the scope of the request, in order to allow the Commission to take appropriate action.

<a id="rec-17"></a>
(17) In accordance with Article 10(3) of Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012, each standardisation request is subject to acceptance by the relevant ESO. It is therefore necessary to provide for the rules on validity of this request if it is not accepted by ETSI.

<a id="rec-18"></a>
(18) In order to ensure legal certainty as to the validity of the request after its execution, it is appropriate to provide for a date of expiry of this Decision.

<a id="rec-19"></a>
(19) The ESOs, the European stakeholders' organisations receiving Union financing, the Commission Expert Group on Radio Equipment and the Telecommunication Conformity Assessment and Market Surveillance Committee established by Article 45 of Directive 2014/53/EU have been consulted.

<a id="rec-20"></a>
(20) The measures provided for in this Decision are in accordance with the opinion of the Committee established by Article 22 of Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

<a id="art-1"></a>
### Article 1 – Requested standardisation activities

1. The European Telecommunications Standards Institute (ETSI) is requested to draft the harmonised standards listed in Annex I to this Decision in support of Article 3(3)(g) of Directive 2014/53/EU, in conjunction with Article 1 of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/320, for hand-held mobile phones with features similar to those of a computer in terms of capability to treat and store data (‘smartphones’) by the deadlines set in that Annex.

2. The standards referred to in the first paragraph shall meet the requirements set out in Annex II.

3. ETSI shall provide the Commission with the titles of the requested harmonised standards in all the official languages of the Union.

<a id="art-2"></a>
### Article 2 – Work programme

1. ETSI shall prepare a work programme indicating all the standards listed in Annex I, the responsible technical bodies and a timetable for the execution of the requested standardisation activities in line with the deadlines set in that Annex.

2. ETSI shall submit the draft work programme to the Commission by 5 December 2020.

3. ETSI shall give the Commission access to an overall project plan containing detailed information on the execution of the requested standardisation activities.

<a id="art-3"></a>
### Article 3 – Reporting

1. ETSI shall report annually to the Commission on the execution of the request referred to in Article 1, indicating the progress made in implementation of the work programme referred to in Article 2.

2. ETSI shall submit the first annual report to the Commission by 5 April 2021. Subsequent annual reports shall be submitted to the Commission by 31 December each year.

3. ETSI shall provide the Commission with the final report by 31 December 2021.

4. ETSI shall promptly report to the Commission any major concerns relating to the scope of the request referred to in Article 1 and the deadlines set out in Annex I.

<a id="art-4"></a>
### Article 4 – Validity of the standardisation request

1. If ETSI does not accept the request referred to in Article 1 within a month of receiving it, the request may not constitute a basis for the standardisation activities referred to in that Article.

2. This Decision shall expire on 31 December 2021.

<a id="art-5"></a>
### Article 5 – Addressee

This Decision is addressed to the European Telecommunications Standards Institute.

Done at Brussels, 2.10.2020

For the Commission Thierry BRETON Member of the Commission

## Annex II – Requirements for the harmonised standards referred to in Article 1(1)

Part A. General requirements

1. Legal requirements to be supported by the harmonised standards

1.1 The harmonised standards, when applied jointly, shall support the application of the requirements relating to access to emergency services set out in Article 3(3)(g) of Directive 2014/53/EU in conjunction with Article 1 of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/320 (“the requirements”). The requirements shall be taken into account from the beginning and throughout the entire process of developing the harmonised standards.

1.2 The harmonised standards shall include the following elements:

- (a) detailed technical specifications of the requirements with regard to the design and manufacture of hand-held mobile telephones with features similar to those of a computer in terms of capability to treat and store data (‘smartphones’) with the purpose of allowing compliance with those requirements;

- (b) test methods and conditions to verify compliance of smartphones with the specifications referred to in point (a);

- (c) any warning information that is to be provided, where such information is necessary to ensure that the compliance of the smartphones with the requirements is not compromised.

1.3 The structure of the harmonised standards shall be such that a clear distinction can be made between those clauses and sub-clauses of the standards which are necessary to be applied in order to benefit from the presumption of conformity and those which are not. In the case where those clauses or sub-clauses, which are not necessary to be applied, are applied they shall not compromise or endanger the conformity with any of the requirements.

1.4 The harmonised standards shall not support any other legal requirements than those set out in Article 3(3)(g) of Directive 2014/53/EU in conjunction with Article 1 of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/320.

1.5 The harmonised standards shall address the elements referred to in point 1.2 in relation to the specifications, test methods and conditions and information and shall not address any other elements, in particular requirements, procedures, responsibilities, or obligations for any economic operator, including notified or other bodies or market surveillance authorities.

1. 6 The harmonised standards shall not:

- (a) make, in their normative part, any references to any of the requirements or procedures set out in Directive 2014/53/EU or Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/320;

- (b) reproduce, in their normative part, any of the requirements or procedures set out in Directive 2014/53/EU or Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/320;

- (c) modify any definitions set out in Directive 2014/53/EU or Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/320;

- (d) define any legally relevant terms not defined in Directive 2014/53/EU or Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/320;

- (e) contradict any provisions of Directive 2014/53/EU or Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/320;

- (f) make conformity with the standards dependent on requirements of an administrative or organisational nature.

2. Legal requirements covered by an individual harmonised standard

2.1 When one of the harmonised standards listed in Annex I does not cover all of the requirements applicable to the products falling under its scope, that standard shall indicate which of the requirements applicable to those products it does not cover.

2.2 Where one of the harmonised standards listed in Annex I contains technical specifications which do not support the application of the requirements, such technical specifications shall be clearly distinguished from the specifications supporting those requirements.

3. Relations with existing standards and standardisation requests under Directive 2014/53/EU

By Implementing Decision C(2015) 5376 ^(2), the Commission made a request to the European Committee for Electrotechnical Standardisation (CENELEC) and ETSI for the drafting and revision of harmonised standards for radio equipment in support of Directive 2014/53/EU. Implementing Decision C(2015) 5376 does not include a request to draft or revise harmonised standards in support of the application of the requirements set out in Article 3(3)(g) of Directive 2014/53/EU in conjunction with Article 1 of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/320. Therefore, the harmonised standards listed in Annex I are not covered by Implementing Decision C(2015) 5376.

Part B. Specific requirements

1. Requirements for all harmonised standards listed in Annex I

1.1 The harmonised standards shall reflect the generally acknowledged state of art, including any relevant techniques proposed in scientific and research literature which represent the state of the art.

1.2 The harmonised standards shall contain verifiable, objective and reproducible methods in a controlled environment to ensure comparable verification of the technical specifications for all the tested smartphones.

2. Requirements for the specific standards listed in Annex I

2.1 Standard A: Harmonised standard on compatibility and interoperability with the Galileo system shall describe solutions that ensure the following:

- (a) usage of caller location solutions based on a global infrastructure composed of at least one constellation of satellites and a network of ground stations, which provides accurate timing and geolocation information;

- (b) capability to receive and process signals from one or more GNSS, and at least from Galileo;

- (c) capability to get a first position fix from any of the processed GNSS signals selected by the smartphone manufacturer, in maximum 60 seconds for signal levels down to minus 130 dBm;

- (d) capability to achieve a horizontal position error of maximum 5 metres in open sky conditions and maximum 25 metres in urban canyon conditions with a confidence level of 95 % (2σ coverage factor), where open sky conditions and urban canyon conditions are as defined, respectively, in points 2.1.8 (Figure 1) and 2.2.4.2 (Figure 3) of Annex VI to Delegated Regulation (EU) 2017/79 ^(3);

- (e) usage of satellite navigation message authentication techniques to detect spoofing attacks.

2.2 Standard B: Harmonised standard on the use of GNSS raw measurements shall describe solutions that ensure the following:

- (a) availability of the GNSS raw measurements made during the location sessions;

- (b) a description of the methodology to make available a minimum set of GNSS raw measurements containing at least satellite identifiers, satellite constellations, carrierto-noise-density ratio, doppler, UTC time and satellites used in the positioning fix.

2.3 Standard C: Harmonised standard on the processing and making available for transmission of the data referred to in Article 1(2) of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/320 shall describe solutions that ensure the following:

- (a) compatibility of the processing and transmission mechanisms of handset-derived caller location with the national Public Safety Answering Point (PSAP) systems deployed;

- (b) transmission of data referred to in Article 1(2) of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/320 through emergency communication, as defined in Article 2(38) of Directive (EU) 2018/1972 of the European Parliament and of the Council ^(4), of all available types.

2.4 Standard D: Harmonised standard on the use of a wireless local area network ^(5) for positioning shall describe solutions that ensure the following:

- (a) capability to implement positioning based on the 802.11 family of standards and existing 802.11 based infrastructure;

- (b) capability to get a first position fix from signals from Wi-Fi Access Points in maximum 20 seconds;

- (c) capability to achieve a horizontal position error of maximum 30 metres in an indoor scenario with a confidence level of 67 % (1σ coverage factor).
