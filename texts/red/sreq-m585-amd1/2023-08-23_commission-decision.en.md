# COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING DECISION of 23.8.2023 amending Implementing Decision C(2022) 5637 on a standardisation request to the European Committee for Standardisation and the European Committee for Electrotechnical Standardisation as regards radio equipment in support of Directive 2014/53/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council and Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/30

COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING DECISION

of 23.8.2023

amending Implementing Decision C(2022) 5637 on a standardisation request to the European Committee for Standardisation and the European Committee for Electrotechnical Standardisation as regards radio equipment in support of Directive 2014/53/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council and Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/30

(Text with EEA relevance)

(Only the English, French and German texts are authentic)

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 October 2012 on European standardisation, amending Council Directives 89/686/EEC and 93/15/EEC and Directives 94/9/EC, 94/25/EC, 95/16/EC, 97/23/EC, 98/34/EC, 2004/22/EC, 2007/23/EC, 2009/23/EC and 2009/105/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Council Decision 87/95/EEC and Decision No 1673/2006/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council ^(1), and in particular Article 10(1) thereof,

Whereas:

## Recitals

<a id="rec-1"></a>
(1) Directive 2014/53/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council ^(2) establishes a regulatory framework for the making available on the market and putting into service in the Union of radio equipment. The essential requirements for such equipment are laid down in Article 3 of that Directive. Pursuant to Article 3(3), second paragraph, of Directive 2014/53/EU, the Commission is to specify which categories or classes of radio equipment are concerned by each of the essential requirements set out in Article 3(3), first subparagraph, points (a) to (i), of that Directive.

<a id="rec-2"></a>
(2) As regards the essential requirements of Directive 2014/53/EU that are set out in Article 3(3), points (d), (e) and (f), of that Directive the Commission adopted Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/30 ^(3). That Delegated Regulation will apply from 1 August 2025.

<a id="rec-3"></a>
(3) In accordance with Article 16 of Directive 2014/53/EU, radio equipment which is in conformity with harmonised standards or parts thereof, the references of which have been published in the Official Journal of the European Union, is to be presumed to be in conformity with the essential requirements set out in Article 3 of that Directive, covered by those standards or parts thereof.

<a id="rec-4"></a>
(4) By Implementing Decision C(2022)5637 ^(4), the Commission made a request to the European Committee for Standardisation (CEN) and the European Committee for Electrotechnical Standardisation (CENELEC) for drafting new harmonised standards in support of Article 3(3), points (d), (e) and (f), of Directive 2014/53/EU, for the categories and classes of the radio equipment specified by Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/30 (‘the request’).

<a id="rec-5"></a>
(5) The matters that need to be addressed by the harmonised standards, relating to protection of network, personal data and privacy and protection from fraud, in support of the essential requirements set out in Article 3(3), points (d), (e) and (f), of Directive 2014/53/EU, regarding the radio equipment concerned by those essential requirements pursuant to Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/30, are very complex.

<a id="rec-6"></a>
(6) It is also the first time that detailed technical specifications, including appropriate test methods or equivalent verifiable and reproducible approaches, in relation to protection of network, personal data and privacy and protection from fraud and regarding the categories and classes of radio equipment specified by Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/30, are being developed.

<a id="rec-7"></a>
(7) CEN and CENELEC have therefore asked for an extension of the period specified in the request, by at least nine months, in order to be able to provide harmonised standards of high quality.

<a id="rec-8"></a>
(8) Various European cybersecurity certification schemes under Regulation (EU) 2019/881 of the European Parliament and of the Council ^(5) are being drafted during the development of harmonised standards in support of Article 3(3), points (d), (e) and (f), of Directive 2014/53/EU, for the categories and classes of the radio equipment specified by Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/30. Those schemes will operate on a voluntary basis and cannot be used to demonstrate conformity to the corresponding essential requirements set out in Directive 2014/53/EU. Nevertheless, as provided for in Implementing Decision C(2022)5637, CEN and CENELEC shall ensure that the relevant harmonised standards are in coherence with the content of those schemes.

<a id="rec-9"></a>
(9) Though the use of harmonised standards under the conformity assessment procedures laid down in Article 17 of Directive 2014/53/EU is not mandatory, it is appropriate to provide in the request for a longer period for the preparation of the harmonised standards in support of Article 3(3), points (d), (e) and (f), of Directive 2014/53/EU, for the categories and classes of the radio equipment specified by Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/30.

<a id="rec-10"></a>
(10) Given that Implementing Decision C(2022)5637, as will be amended via this Decision, will expire on 31 December 2025, Article 3 (4) of Implementing Decision C(2022)5637 should provide that the final report that shall be submitted by CEN and CENELEC to the Commission shall be submitted by that date, i.e. by 31 December 2025, and not by 31 December 2027.

<a id="rec-11"></a>
(11) Implementing Decision C(2022)5637 should therefore be amended accordingly.

<a id="rec-12"></a>
(12) The European standardisation organisations, the European stakeholders' organisations receiving Union financing, the Commission Expert Group on Radio Equipment and the Telecommunication Conformity Assessment and Market Surveillance Committee established by Article 45 of Directive 2014/53/EU have been consulted.

<a id="rec-13"></a>
(13) In accordance with Article 10(3) of Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012, each standardisation request is subject to acceptance by the relevant European standardisation organisation. It is therefore necessary to lay down rules on the validity of this Decision in the event that CEN or CENELEC does not accept the amendments to Implementing Decision C(2022)5637 set out in this Decision.

<a id="rec-14"></a>
(14) The measures provided for in this Decision are in accordance with the opinion of the Committee established by Article 22 of Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

<a id="art-1"></a>
### Article 1

Implementing Decision C(2022)5637 is amended as follows:

- (1) in Article 3(4), ‘2027’ is replaced by ‘2025’;

- (2) in Article 4, second subparagraph, ‘30 September 2024’ is replaced by ‘31 December 2025’;

- (3) in entries 1, 2 and 3 of the Table in Annex I, ‘30 September 2023’ is replaced by ‘30 June 2024’.

<a id="art-2"></a>
### Article 2

If the European Committee for Standardisation or the European Committee for Electrotechnical Standardisation indicates that it does not accept the amendments set out in Article 1 of this Decision, this Decision shall cease to apply and Implementing Decision C(2022)5637 in its version of 5 August 2022 shall continue to apply.

<a id="art-3"></a>
### Article 3

This Decision is addressed to the European Committee for Standardisation and the European Committee for Electrotechnical Standardisation.

Done at Brussels, 23.8.2023

For the Commission Thierry BRETON Member of the Commission
