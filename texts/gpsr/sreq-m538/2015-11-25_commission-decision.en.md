THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 October 2012 on European standardisation, amending Council Directives 89/686/EEC and 93/15/EEC and Directives 94/9/EC, 94/25/EC, 95/16/EC, 97/23/EC, 98/34/EC, 2004/22/EC, 2007/23/EC, 2009/23/EC and 2009/105/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Council Decision 87/95/EEC and Decision No 1673/2006/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council ^(1), and in particular Article 10(1) thereof,

Having regard to Directive 2001/95/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 3 December 2001 on general product safety ^(2), and in particular Article 4(1)(b) thereof,

Whereas:

## Recitals

<a id="rec-1"></a>
(1) The procedure for adopting standardisation requests is laid down in Article 10(2) of Regulation (EU) 1025/2012. The European standardisation bodies should draw up standards in accordance with the requirements determined in accordance with Article 4(1)(a) of Directive 2001/95/EC, on a request from the Commission.

<a id="rec-2"></a>
(2) Commission Decision 2015/547/EU ^(3) lays down specific safety requirements for alcohol-powered flueless fireplaces for the purposes of the standards referred to in Article 4(1) of Directive 2001/95/EC.

<a id="rec-3"></a>
(3) It has already been identified, through Commission Communications ^(4), that there should be European standards for alcohol-powered flueless fireplaces in order to address the risks related to their use, particularly as regards design and manufacturing.

<a id="rec-4"></a>
(4) The European standardisation organisations, the European stakeholder organisations receiving Union financing, and the Committee established by Article 15 of Directive 2001/95/EC have been consulted.

<a id="rec-5"></a>
(5) The European standardisation organisations have indicated that the activities related to the Commission's request fall entirely under the scope of the European Committee for Standardisation ('CEN').

<a id="rec-6"></a>
(6) The measures provided for in this Decision are in accordance with the opinion of the Committee established by Article 22 of Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

<a id="art-1"></a>
### Article 1 – Standardisation request

1. The European Committee for Standardisation ('CEN') is requested to draft a European standard or standards for alcohol-powered flueless fireplaces meeting the requirements set out in Commission Decision 2015/547/EU.

2. As regards emissions referred to in point 1.4. of the Annex to Decision 2015/547/EU, CEN shall take into account the final report of the 'Study on alcohol-powered flueless fireplace combustion and its effects on indoor air quality' produced by the consortium partners Innovhub-SSI, Kiwa Italia and Politecnico Milano commissioned by the European Commission under contract number 2013 86 03.

<a id="art-2"></a>
### Article 2 – Work programme

1. CEN shall prepare a work programme indicating the requested standard or standards, the responsible technical bodies and a tentative timetable for the drafting of the standard or standards requested in Article 1, respecting the deadlines set out in Article 3. CEN shall submit the work programme to the Commission by 1 February 2016 and shall provide access to an overall project plan to the Commission.

2. CEN shall update the work programme as appropriate during the work on the draft standard or standards and inform the Commission of any amendments to the work programme.

<a id="art-3"></a>
### Article 3 – Time schedule and reporting

1. CEN shall adopt the European standard or standards requested in Article 1 by 1 December 2018.

2. CEN shall submit to the Commission annual reports on the execution of the request referred to in Article 1 as follows:

- (a) a first annual report by 1 December 2016;

- (b) a second annual report by 1 December 2017; and

- (c) a final report by 1 December 2018

<a id="art-4"></a>
### Article 4 – General requirements

1. CEN shall include in the standard or standards requested in Article 1, a clear and precise indication with regard to the relationship between its content and the corresponding requirements of Decision 2015/547/EU.

2. The standard or standards requested in Article 1 shall make reference to this Decision.

3. If revisions or amendments of the standard or standards requested in Article 1 are subsequently adopted, CEN shall include, in those revisions or amendments, information about significant changes introduced by each revised or amended standard.

4. CEN shall provide the Commission with the titles of the standard or standards requested in Article 1, in all the official languages of the Union.

<a id="art-5"></a>
### Article 5 – Validity of the request

If the request referred to in Article 1 is not accepted by CEN within one month following its receipt, that request shall not constitute a basis for the drafting of standards.

<a id="art-6"></a>
### Article 6 – Addressee

This Decision is addressed to the European Committee for Standardisation.

Done at Brussels, 24.11.2015
