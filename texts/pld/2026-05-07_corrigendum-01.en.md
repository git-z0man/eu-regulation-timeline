# Corrigendum to Directive (EU) 2024/2853 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 23 October 2024 on liability for defective products and repealing Council Directive 85/374/EEC ( Official Journal of the European Union L, 2024/2853, 18 November 2024 )

On page 11, Article 2(1):

- for: ‘1. This Directive shall apply to products placed on the market or put into service after 9 December 2026.’

  ,

- read: ‘1. This Directive shall apply to products placed on the market or put into service after 8 December 2026.’.
