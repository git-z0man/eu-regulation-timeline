About

The EU regulation timeline is a private spare-time project by Steffen Zimmermann. It follows the EU's digital and product legislation (the Cyber Resilience Act, NIS-2, the AI Act, the Machinery Regulation, the European Product Act and the acts around them) from the official sources, and turns them into a timeline, procedure pages, standards overviews and a forecast of when open procedures are likely to become law.

It is independent: it does not speak for any employer, association or institution, and it is not legal advice. The legal texts on EUR-Lex prevail.
