MDR/IVDR simplification proposal amends this act

Proposal for a Regulation amending Regulations (EU) 2017/745 and (EU) 2017/746 as regards simplifying and reducing the burden of the rules on medical devices and in vitro diagnostic medical devices

COM(2025) 1023 (proposal) · proposed 16 Dec 2025 · Legislative procedure →

Why it is listed

Would move the Medical Devices Regulation (EU) 2017/745 and the IVD Regulation (EU) 2017/746 from Section A to Section B of Annex I AI Act (Art. 4 of the proposal). For products under Section B, only Art. 6(1), Art. 60a and Art. 102 to 112 AI Act apply (Art. 2(2) AI Act as amended by the Digital Omnibus on AI); the high-risk requirements would be taken into account through the medical devices rules.

Digital Omnibus on AI adopted amends this act

Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 amending Regulations (EU) 2024/1689, (EU) 2018/1139 and (EU) 2023/1230 as regards the simplification of the implementation of harmonised rules on artificial intelligence (Digital Omnibus on AI)

Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 · published 24 Jul 2026 · Legislative procedure →

Why it is listed

Adopted, not pending. Amends the AI Act in 43 points to simplify its implementation; among others it replaces Art. 4 (AI literacy), moves the obligations for high-risk AI systems to 2 December 2027 (Annex III) and 2 August 2028 (Annex I) (Art. 113 as amended) and gives providers of generative AI systems placed on the market before 2 August 2026 until 2 December 2026 to comply with Art. 50(2) (Art. 111(4)).

Planned (annual Union work programme)

Open an action for its text in the work programme.

  • 2026, Action 1: Artificial intelligence
    Legal reference
    Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 laying down harmonised rules on artificial intelligence (Artificial Intelligence Act)
    Planned deliverables
    Preparation of standardisation request to develop European standards and/or European standardisation deliverables in support of Article 40 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (Artificial Intelligence Act), covering: (i) reporting and documentation processes to improve the resource performance of artificial intelligence systems (including high-risk systems), with lifecycle measurement methods, metrics, boundary conditions and reporting templates for energy and other resource consumption; and (ii) deliverables on the energy-efficient development of general-purpose AI models (development, training and inference). The request complements, without duplicating, work already mandated by Commission Implementing Decision C(2025) 3871, standardisation request M/613. It excludes essential requirements for high-risk AI systems covered by M/613. Outputs will build on ongoing environmental-impact work and inform the Commission’s review under Article 112(6).
    Objective
    Promote human-centric, trustworthy AI that protects health, safety, fundamental rights, democracy, the rule of law and the environment, while limiting harmful effects and supporting innovation.
