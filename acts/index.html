Topic · as of 29 Sep 2026

High-risk suppliers

"High-risk supplier" is on its way to becoming a horizontal eligibility filter in EU law: it would decide who may supply key ICT assets of essential and important entities, take part in public procurement and auctions, receive public support and EU funding, and take part in European standardisation, certification and conformity assessment.

Where it applies: Definition and identification, Operators of key ICT assets (NIS-2 entities), Public procurement, Auctions and public support schemes, EU funding, European standardisation and common specifications, Certification and conformity assessment, Market surveillance of products
Topic · as of 11 Oct 2026

New Standardisation Regulation – routes to presumption of conformity

On 6 October 2026 the Commission proposed a new Standardisation Regulation (COM(2026) 780) to replace Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012.

Where it applies: Instruments of presumption (harmonised standards, deliverables, common specifications), Standardisation requests and deadlines, Who develops the standards, Assessment, citation and withdrawal, Access to standards, Participation and inclusiveness, Financing of the standardisation system, International standardisation, Governance, entry into application and transition
