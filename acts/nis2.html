NIS-2 amendment

Directive amending Directive (EU) 2022/2555 as regards simplification measures and alignment with the Cybersecurity Act 2

COM(2026) 13 · proposed 20 Jan 2026 · Legislative procedure →

Why it is listed

Would amend NIS-2 in 13 points for simplification and alignment with the Cybersecurity Act 2 (CSA2): among others it widens the scope (e.g. providers of European Digital Identity and Business Wallets; strategic dual-use infrastructure regardless of size, Art. 2), replaces Art. 5, adds paragraphs to the reporting rules in Art. 23 and 24 and amends Annexes I and II.

Digital Omnibus

Regulation amending Regulations (EU) 2016/679, (EU) 2018/1724, (EU) 2018/1725, (EU) 2023/2854 and Directives 2002/58/EC, (EU) 2022/2555 and (EU) 2022/2557 as regards the simplification of the digital legislative framework

COM(2025) 837 · proposed 19 Nov 2025 · Legislative procedure →

Why it is listed

Would add a single-entry point for incident reporting run by ENISA and usable under several Union acts (new Art. 23a NIS-2), let a manufacturer's notification of a severe incident under Art. 14(3) CRA also serve the NIS-2 reporting where it contains the required information (Art. 23), and allow voluntary notifications through the single-entry point (Art. 30(1)).
