Omnibus IV – Digitalisation and common specifications (Regulation)

Regulation amending Regulations (EU) No 765/2008, (EU) 2016/424, (EU) 2016/425, (EU) 2016/426, (EU) 2023/1230, (EU) 2023/1542 and (EU) 2024/1781 as regards digitalisation and common specifications

COM(2025) 504 · proposed 21 May 2025 · Legislative procedure →

Why it is listed

Would digitalise information duties in the Machinery Regulation: a 'digital contact' of economic operators (new Art. 3(22a)), the EU declaration of conformity through an internet address or machine- readable code (Art. 10(8)), documentation for authorities and notified bodies in digital format (Art. 10(10), 25(6)) and a link to a digital product passport where other legislation requires one (Art. 21(5)). Under the provisional agreement of June 2026 (agreed text PE790.900, not law), these amendments apply 30 months after entry into force (Art. 9), not from 20 January 2027.

Digital Omnibus on AI

Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 amending Regulations (EU) 2024/1689, (EU) 2018/1139 and (EU) 2023/1230 as regards the simplification of the implementation of harmonised rules on artificial intelligence (Digital Omnibus on AI)

Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 · published 24 Jul 2026 · Legislative procedure →

Why it is listed

Empowers the Commission to add health and safety requirements for AI systems that are high-risk under Art. 6(1) AI Act to Annex III of the Machinery Regulation by delegated act, applying by 2 August 2028 (Art. 8 and 47 amended), and adds a transitional rule on the harmonised standards and common specifications of the AI Act for such systems (new Art. 20(10)).

Regulation (EU) 2024/2748 amending Regulations (EU) No 305/2011, (EU) 2016/424, (EU) 2016/425, (EU) 2016/426, (EU) 2023/988 and (EU) 2023/1230 as regards emergency procedures for the conformity assessment, presumption of conformity, adoption of common specifications and market surveillance due to an internal market emergency

Regulation (EU) 2024/2748 · published 8 Nov 2024 · Legislative procedure →

Why it is listed

Inserts Chapter IVa (emergency procedures, Art. 25a to 25e): only while an internal market emergency mode is activated under Regulation (EU) 2024/2747 and for machinery designated as crisis-relevant goods, it allows emergency procedures for conformity assessment, presumption of conformity, common specifications and market surveillance.

Steps (Start NWI · Drafting prEN · Voting prEN · Drafting EN · Voting EN · Adoption EN · HAS process · Citation hEN): the step a project has reached, after the status on its public CEN-CENELEC or ETSI page (as in DKE's overview of the CRA projects): filled = done, arrow = current, hatched = no citation expected for this act. A published standard is in the HAS process until it is cited in the Official Journal. Our reading of the public status, not an official stage.

Text expected: the quarter in which the CEN-CENELEC project plan expects the definitive text (DAV), the step before the Commission can cite a harmonised standard in the Official Journal. Rounded; a forecast of the technical body, not a commitment. Plan outdated: the plan's next step is more than 90 days overdue, so its dates do not reflect the current progress.

Annex Z: whether the draft must contain an Annex Z(A) that relates its clauses to the essential requirements; without it, a standard cannot give a presumption of conformity. Citation: whether the standard is a candidate for citation in the Official Journal. Commission assessment: the result of the Commission's latest assessment of the draft against the standardisation request (phase: ENQ enquiry, FV formal vote). All three as recorded by CEN-CENELEC for the project; they can change until the standard is cited.

  • 2026, Action 34: Anthropometric considerations for personal protective equipment
    Legal reference
    Regulation (EU) 2016/425 on personal protective equipment Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 on machinery
    Planned deliverables
    Prenormative work to strengthen the inclusiveness of European standards for machinery and personal protective equipment in support of Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 and Regulation (EU) 2016/425. The Commission will request expert mentoring in anthropometrics to relevant technical committees and to develop horizontal guidance deliverables. Activities will include expert-led workshops, ad hoc advice during drafting, and structured feedback on drafts, plus practical checklists for design and test methods that accommodate a wider range of body sizes, shapes and capabilities. Guidance will cover user panels, sampling strategies, adjustability, fit/comfort metrics and documentation.
    Objective
    Improve the inclusiveness of machinery and personal protective equipment standards by embedding anthropometric considerations into routine drafting and review so that revisions better reflect diverse users.

WIPANO – knowledge and technology transfer through patents and standards

Germany · Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Energy (BMWE) · administered by Projektträger Jülich · Richtlinie vom 8. Januar 2024, BAnz AT 02.02.2024 B1 · status on its page: “Einreichung möglich”

Funds companies taking part in national, European and international standardisation committees (DIN/DKE, CEN/CENELEC, ETSI, ISO/IEC), where the harmonised standards under these acts are written.

  • 3.1 Standardisation – companies: Active work in up to two standardisation committees at a time not joined in the last three years (also through a mandated third party); flat rates per meeting (European: 1,500 EUR in person, 750 EUR virtual), standards management, a DIN SPEC or VDE SPEC. Up to 45,000 EUR, 70 % of the expenses, up to 36 months. Who: Companies with up to 1,000 staff and 100 million EUR turnover, with a site in Germany; de minimis. Applications until 31 May 2027.
  • 3.2 Knowledge transfer through standardisation: Preparing research results for standardisation, R&D on digital standardisation; at least one standardisation proposal. Up to 200,000 EUR per partner; companies usually up to 50 %, SMEs up to 80 %, research up to 85 %. Who: Consortia of companies, standards bodies and their committees, universities and research organisations in Germany. Sketches until 31 May 2026 (passed), applications until 31 May 2027.
  • 2.1 Patenting – companies: A first patent application. Up to 50 %; module 1 up to 10,000 EUR, module 2 up to 6,000 EUR. Who: SMEs. Applications until 31 Oct 2027.
