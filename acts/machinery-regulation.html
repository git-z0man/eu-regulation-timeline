Omnibus IV – Digitalisation and common specifications (Regulation) proposal amends this act

Proposal for a Regulation amending Regulations (EU) No 765/2008, (EU) 2016/424, (EU) 2016/425, (EU) 2016/426, (EU) 2023/1230, (EU) 2023/1542 and (EU) 2024/1781 as regards digitalisation and common specifications

COM(2025) 504 (proposal) · proposed 21 May 2025 · Legislative procedure →

Why it is listed

Would digitalise information duties in the Machinery Regulation: a 'digital contact' of economic operators (new Art. 3(22a)), the EU declaration of conformity through an internet address or machine- readable code (Art. 10(8)), documentation for authorities and notified bodies in digital format (Art. 10(10), 25(6)) and a link to a digital product passport where other legislation requires one (Art. 21(5)). Part of the Omnibus IV package, which changes several product regulations at once.

Digital Omnibus on AI adopted amends this act

Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 amending Regulations (EU) 2024/1689, (EU) 2018/1139 and (EU) 2023/1230 as regards the simplification of the implementation of harmonised rules on artificial intelligence (Digital Omnibus on AI)

Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 · published 24 Jul 2026 · Legislative procedure →

Why it is listed

Adopted, not pending. Empowers the Commission to add health and safety requirements for AI systems that are high-risk under Art. 6(1) AI Act to Annex III of the Machinery Regulation by delegated act, applying by 2 August 2028 (Art. 8 and 47 amended), and adds a transitional rule on the harmonised standards and common specifications of the AI Act for such systems (new Art. 20(10)).

Regulation (EU) 2024/2748 · published 08 Nov 2024 · Legislative procedure →

Why it is listed

Adopted, not pending. Inserts Chapter IVa (emergency procedures, Art. 25a to 25e): only while an internal market emergency mode is activated under Regulation (EU) 2024/2747 and for machinery designated as crisis-relevant goods, it allows emergency procedures for conformity assessment, presumption of conformity, common specifications and market surveillance.

The Commission will request expert mentoring in anthropometrics to relevant technical committees and to develop horizontal guidance deliverables. Activities will include expert-led workshops, ad hoc advice during drafting, and structured feedback on drafts, plus practical checklists for design and test methods that accommodate a wider range of body sizes, shapes and capabilities. Guidance will cover user panels, sampling strategies, adjustability, fit/comfort metrics and documentation.
    Objective
    Improve the inclusiveness of machinery and personal protective equipment standards by embedding anthropometric considerations into routine drafting and review so that revisions better reflect diverse users.
