Dates and obligations

Show on the timeline
What it means for a machinery builder: Market surveillance of machinery and other CE-marked products. A machine under the Machinery Directive (listed in Art. 4(5) MSR) may be placed on the market only with an economic operator established in the Union who answers to the authorities (Art. 4 MSR), also for online sales. To be merged into the European Product Act.
