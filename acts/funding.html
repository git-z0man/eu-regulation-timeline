National programmes

WIPANO – knowledge and technology transfer through patents and standards

Germany · Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Energy (BMWE) · administered by Projektträger Jülich · Richtlinie vom 8. Januar 2024, BAnz AT 02.02.2024 B1 · status on its page: “Einreichung möglich”

Funds companies taking part in national, European and international standardisation committees (DIN/DKE, CEN/CENELEC, ETSI, ISO/IEC), where the harmonised standards under these acts are written.

  • 3.1 Standardisation – companies: Active work in up to two standardisation committees at a time not joined in the last three years (also through a mandated third party); flat rates per meeting (European: 1,500 EUR in person, 750 EUR virtual), standards management, a DIN SPEC or VDE SPEC. Up to 45,000 EUR, 70 % of the expenses, up to 36 months. Who: Companies with up to 1,000 staff and 100 million EUR turnover, with a site in Germany; de minimis. Applications until 31 May 2027.
  • 3.2 Knowledge transfer through standardisation: Preparing research results for standardisation, R&D on digital standardisation; at least one standardisation proposal. Up to 200,000 EUR per partner; companies usually up to 50 %, SMEs up to 80 %, research up to 85 %. Who: Consortia of companies, standards bodies and their committees, universities and research organisations in Germany. Sketches until 31 May 2026 (passed), applications until 31 May 2027.
  • 2.1 Patenting – companies: A first patent application. Up to 50 %; module 1 up to 10,000 EUR, module 2 up to 6,000 EUR. Who: SMEs. Applications until 31 Oct 2027.

IT security in business – transfer centre for cybersecurity and focus projects

Germany · Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Energy (BMWE) · administered by DLR Projektträger · Förderbekanntmachung vom 15. September 2022 (BAnz AT 23.09.2022 B1), zuletzt geändert am 29. April 2026 (BAnz AT 12.05.2026 B1)

Funds projects that raise the IT security of SMEs, craft businesses and start-ups (the Transferstelle Cybersicherheit im Mittelstand and focus projects); companies use their offers free of charge.

State: Projektskizzen: the deadline of 1 Aug 2026 is suspended (third amendment of 29 Apr 2026); the deadlines of 1 Feb 2025 and 1 Aug 2025 were suspended too.

Central Innovation Programme for SMEs (ZIM)

Germany · Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Energy (BMWE)

Topic-open grants for research and development of SMEs, alone or with partners; a product's security work for the CRA can be such a project.

State: Applications stopped since 7 Jul 2026 (all project forms and sketches, except calls with international partners); a restart is aimed for early 2027, depending on the federal budget 2027. Reported by the chambers of commerce; zim.de cannot be read automatically.

Sovereign Tech Fund (Sovereign Tech Agency)

Germany · Sovereign Tech Agency (Germany) · status on its page: “Go to the application platform”

Invests worldwide in the maintenance and security of open software components that underpin digital infrastructure; open source stewards and maintainers apply through the agency's platform (answer within ten weeks).

Digitalbonus.Bayern

Bavaria · Bavarian State Ministry of Economic Affairs (StMWi) · administered by the Bavarian district governments · status on its page: “Bis Dezember 2027 können kleine Unternehmen wieder Anträge stellen”

Grants for small companies (fewer than 50 staff, at most 10 million EUR turnover or balance sheet total) with a site in Bavaria; funding area 2.2 IT security: introducing or improving IT security processes, with the hardware needed (firewall, backup, network security).

  • Digitalbonus Standard: Products, processes and services, IT security (funding area 2.2); from 4,000 EUR of eligible expenses; a monthly quota, applications through an ELSTER company account. Up to 7,500 EUR, 50 %. Who: Small companies of the commercial economy in Bavaria. Applications until 31 Dec 2027.
  • Digitalbonus Plus: Measures of particular innovation. Up to 30,000 EUR, 50 %. Who: Small companies of the commercial economy in Bavaria. Applications until 31 Dec 2027.

Mittelstand Innovativ & Digital (MID) – digital security

North Rhine-Westphalia · State of North Rhine-Westphalia · administered by NRW.BANK (since 1 Jan 2026) · Richtlinie zur Durchführung des Förderprogramms Mittelstand Innovativ & Digital (MB.NRW 2026 Nr. 12) · status on its page: “Eine Registrierung zur Verlosung ist möglich”

Grants for micro, small and medium-sized companies in North Rhine-Westphalia to raise their digital security: analysis by an external IT service provider, training of staff, software and hardware for basic IT protection (antivirus, patch management, firewall).

  • Digitale Sicherheit: A monthly lottery among registered companies, then the application; measures of 3 to 12 months. 50 %, up to 15,000 EUR, from 4,000 EUR. Who: SMEs (EU definition) with their seat in North Rhine-Westphalia, once in two years. Applications open.
