PPA proposal amends this act

Proposal for a Regulation on public contracts and concessions, repealing Directives 2014/23/EU, 2014/24/EU and 2014/25/EU

COM(2026) 590 (proposal) · proposed 09 Sep 2026 · Legislative procedure →

Why it is listed

Would delete Art. 5 CRA (procurement or use of products with digital elements) and move its rule into the Public Procurement Act: Art. 68 of the proposal makes Member States ensure that compliance with the essential cybersecurity requirements of Annex I CRA is taken into account when such products are procured. References to Art. 5 CRA would be read as references to the PPA (Art. 147(11); the correlation table of the proposal names Art. 67). The CRA is one of 13 acts whose procurement rules the PPA consolidates (recital 63).

Article 147 – Amendments to horizontal public procurement provisions (Commission proposal, PPA)

  • (11) Article 5 of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 is deleted.
  • References to Article 5 of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 shall be construed as references to Article 68 of this Regulation;

Regulation (EU) 2025/327 · published 05 Mar 2025 · Legislative procedure →

Why it is listed

Adopted, not pending. Electronic health record (EHR) systems that are products with digital elements show conformity with the essential requirements of the CRA through the conformity assessment of the EHDS (new Art. 32(5a) CRA); one cybersecurity risk assessment and one set of technical documentation cover both acts (Art. 13(4) and 31(3) CRA replaced). The EHDS applies from 26 March 2027 (Art. 105 EHDS).

Horizontal standards (EN 40000-1 series, CEN/CLC/JTC 13)

The horizontal standards in support of the CRA: vocabulary, principles and risk management, vulnerability handling, and the generic security requirements of Annex I, Part I, point (2) CRA. Only the standard for the vulnerability handling requirements of Annex I, Part II (EN 40000-1-3) is intended for citation in the Official Journal and can thus give a presumption of conformity (Art. 27(1) CRA). The standards for the requirements of Annex I, Part I (EN 40000-1-2, the future EN 40000-1-4) and the vocabulary are harmonised standards requested by M/606 but not intended for citation: the CEN-CENELEC project pages say “No citation expected”, and they have no Annex ZA. Manufacturers can apply them, but they give no presumption of conformity.

  • 2026, Action 5: Cybersecurity requirements for products with digital elements
    Legal reference
    Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 on horizontal cybersecurity requirements for products with digital elements (Cyber Resilience Act)
    Planned deliverables
    Standardisation request to develop harmonised European standards in support of Regulation (EU) 2024/8247 (Cyber Resilience Act), complementing Commission Implementing Decision C(2025) 618, standardisation request M/606. While M/606 mandates horizontal cybersecurity properties and vertical standards for Annex III/IV critical products, this request addresses gaps for products with digital elements outside those annexes, notably in rail, machinery, lifts, agricultural machinery, artificial intelligence systems and solar inverters. Deliverables will implement the Act’s essential requirements in these domains, including test methods and conformity evidence. Standards will align with Union legislation, delimit scope to avoid overlap, and facilitate publication of references in the Official Journal of the EU.
    Objective
    Enable secure products with digital elements outside CRA Annexes III–IV through harmonised standards that confer presumption of conformity with Annex I and support lifecycle cybersecurity obligations.
