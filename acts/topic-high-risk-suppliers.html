High-risk suppliers

"High-risk supplier" is on its way to becoming a horizontal eligibility filter in EU law: it would decide who may supply key ICT assets of essential and important entities, take part in public procurement and auctions, receive public support and EU funding, and take part in European standardisation, certification and conformity assessment. No act in force defines the term. The only definition is in the Commission proposal for a Cybersecurity Act 2 (Art. 2(39)); the proposals for an Industrial Accelerator Act and a Public Procurement Act already refer to it. The law in force works one step earlier, with non-technical risk factors (NIS-2, Cyber Resilience Act) and third-country jurisdiction tests in renewable energy auctions (Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1176).

Our own assessment as of 29 Sep 2026; not legal advice. Only the law in force is binding. Proposals and negotiation drafts are not law and can still change; a draft report or a compromise text is the position of one rapporteur or Presidency, not of the institution.

Definition and identification

NIS-2: Art. 22(1)

Official JournalIdentification

Coordinated security risk assessments of critical ICT supply chains by the NIS Cooperation Group, taking into account technical and non-technical risk factors: the procedure CSA2 builds on.

Official source · act page · Text: EN · DE (en.md de.md)

BSIG: § 2 Nr. 23, § 56(6)

Consolidated versionIdentification

'Critical components': ICT products designated by ordinance per sector, if used in critical facilities, realising a critical function, and a disruption could impair their functioning or public order or security. The German counterpart of key ICT assets.

Official source

CSA2: Art. 2(37)-(43)

Commission proposalDefinition

'High-risk supplier': an entity established in, or controlled from, a third country designated as posing cybersecurity concerns (Art. 100), or an entity designated individually (Art. 103(7)). 'Control' means the ability to exercise a decisive influence.

Official source · act page · Text: EN · DE (en.md de.md)

CSA2: Art. 99

Commission proposalIdentification

Union-level coordinated security risk assessments, requested by the Commission or at least three Member States, within six months.

Official source · act page · Text: EN · DE (en.md de.md)

CSA2: Art. 100(1)-(3)

Commission proposalIdentification

Designation of third countries posing cybersecurity concerns by implementing act: laws or practices requiring vulnerabilities to be disclosed before exploitation, no effective judicial remedies, state-controlled threat actors, coordinated risk assessments.

Official source · act page · Text: EN · DE (en.md de.md)

CSA2: Art. 102-107

Commission proposalIdentification

Key ICT assets and lists of high-risk suppliers by implementing acts; individual designation; exemptions; register.

Official source · act page · Text: EN · DE (en.md de.md)

CSA2: Am 26-28, 97-105, 161-164

EP draft report (rapporteur)Definition

Rapporteur's draft report: supplier-by-supplier assessment with an evaluation matrix (country, control, supplier, mitigation, presumptions) instead of country designation; lists by delegated act (Am 111).

BSIG: § 41

Consolidated versionPrecursor

Federal Ministry of the Interior may prohibit, supplier by supplier, the use of critical components in critical facilities where public order or security is likely to be impaired; criteria include control by, or duties to cooperate with, a third-country government (Abs. 4 Nr. 1) and trustworthiness (Nr. 3). Extension to all operators by general order (Abs. 2 Nr. 2). No HRS term; the national counterpart of CSA2 Art. 103.

Official source

CSA2: Art. 103, 110-111, Annex II

Commission proposalConsequence

Prohibition with phase-out, or mitigating measures, for key ICT assets of NIS-2 entities; mobile networks phase out high-risk suppliers' components within 36 months of the list.

Official source · act page · Text: EN · DE (en.md de.md)

CSA2: Art. 115(5)-(7)

Commission proposalConsequence

Public procurement

CSA2: Art. 100(4)(e)

Commission proposalConsequence

No participation in public procurement for ICT components of key ICT assets.

Official source · act page · Text: EN · DE (en.md de.md)

PPA: Art. 66(5)

Commission proposalCross-reference

Public buyers shall exclude, at any stage of the procedure, an operator identified as a high-risk supplier under CSA2 for ICT components of key ICT assets (the cross-reference to the CSA2 article is left blank).

Official source · Text: EN (en.md)

PPA: Art. 66(1)-(4), (6)

Commission proposalIdentification

A security test of its own for every contract, independent of CSA2: risks from third-country dependency and state-controlled influence, and from an operator's ownership, control or financing and its exposure to third-country laws that compel disclosure; met through specifications, selection and award criteria or restricted invitations; harmonised criteria by delegated act.

Official source · Text: EN (en.md)

PPA: Art. 67, 131(4), 134(2)

Commission proposalConsequence

Termination of the contract or exclusion during performance when a security risk materialises (written assessment, hearing of at least ten days); eProcurement providers and the national procurement data spaces must be owned and controlled from the EEA, with no decisive influence from a third country.

Official source · Text: EN (en.md)

PPA: Art. 72(1)(b)-(c), 75; Recital 34

Commission proposalIdentification

Country level: by delegated act the Commission may treat operators and goods of certain third countries as not covered to avoid dependencies threatening security of supply or for economic security, and extend European preference to non-covered operators; recital 34 cites the risk of 'high-risk entities' in sensitive supply chains.

Official source · Text: EN (en.md)

CADA: Art. 17-18, Annex II

Commission proposalPrecursor

Auctions and public support schemes

NZIA: Art. 26(1)(a)(ii), 26(2)

Official JournalPrecursor

Cyber security and data security as pre-qualification criteria in renewable energy auctions (from 30 December 2025), and a resilience criterion on supply from a single third country.

Official source · Text: EN · DE (en.md de.md)

Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1176: Art. 5

Official JournalPrecursor

Bidders or ICT suppliers under the jurisdiction of a third country that requires vulnerabilities to be reported before exploitation, or linked to malicious cyber activity by a public EU or Member State statement: cybersecurity plan, data kept in the EEA, operational control by an EEA operator. The same triggers as the country criteria of CSA2.

Official source · Text: EN · DE (en.md de.md)

IAA: Art. 34 (NZIA Art. 26(1)(a)(iv), 28b, 28c(3))

Commission proposalCross-reference

High-risk suppliers as defined in CSA2 excluded from control, SCADA, remote access and firewall systems in all auctions for net-zero technologies, from public support schemes and from State support to manufacturing.
