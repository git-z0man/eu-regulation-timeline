Dates and obligations

Show on the timeline
What it means for a machinery builder: Proposed 6 Oct 2026 (COM(2026) 780), replaces Reg. 1025/2012 six months after entry into force (Art. 43; entry into force expected in 2028), without transitional rules. Free online access to referenced standards (Art. 5); interim deliverables, designated standards development organisations and common specifications when the ESOs are late (Arts. 2(7), 29, 32). M/606 (CRA) expires before, M/605 phase 2 (machinery, due 2034) runs across the switch; Art. 20 MR and Art. 27 CRA still name only harmonised standards.
