## About

Tasks:

- The group supports ENISA’s activities in: putting forward analyses, recommendations and good practices that ensure appropriate cybersecurity levels of digital products and services during their planning, design, development, and maintenance; the maintenance of the EU Vulnerability Database, its cooperation with the Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures (CVE) Program, and potential other activities that contribute to serving its stakeholders and providing the expected value based on a delivery of matured services.
