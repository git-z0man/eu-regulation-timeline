## About

- Type: Informal, Permanent, Limited
- Policy areas: Consumer affairs
- Registered: 2005-09-29; last updated: 2026-08-31
- Link: <http://ec.europa.eu/consumers/consumers_safety/cooperation_with_stakeholders/index_en.htm>

Tasks:

- Coordinate with Member States, exchange of views
- Provides expertise to the Commission when preparing implementing measures, i.e. before the Commission submits these draft measures to a comitology committee
