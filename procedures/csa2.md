## Notes

- Status as of 25 Sep 2026: first reading, committee stage in the EP; no Council mandate for negotiations (not public). No trilogue has taken place.
- The Directive amending NIS 2 (COM(2026) 13, procedure 2026/0012(COD)) is negotiated together with this Regulation in the Council (ST 9399/26 covers both).
- Several Member States asked the Council Legal Service to examine the legal basis (ST 9399/26, point 16).
