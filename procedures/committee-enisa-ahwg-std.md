## About

Tasks:

- The Agency invites experts with strong knowledge and experience in cybersecurity standardisation. Members will support ENISA in drafting specific reports on standards and contributing to a comprehensive inventory of cybersecurity-related standards (existing, emerging, and planned). They will also help identify and map relevant standardisation initiatives from European, national, international, and industry bodies, and review or provide feedback on sector-specific gap and overlap analyses of cybersecurity standards when requested.
