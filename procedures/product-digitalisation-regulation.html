17 Feb 2026A10-0024/2026EP committee reportReport on the proposal for a regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council amending Regulations (EU) No 765/2008, (EU) 2016/424, (EU) 2016/425, (EU) …
Our summary: Report of the European Parliament's Committee on the Internal Market and Consumer Protection (IMCO) on the Omnibus IV Regulation on digitalisation and common specifications (COM(2025) 504), adopted in committee on 27 January 2026 and tabled for plenary on 17 February 2026, with the Environment committee's opinion annexed; it is the committee's position, not Parliament's and not law. Its 85 amendments tie the Commission's new power to adopt common specifications for cableways, personal protective equipment and gas appliances to a failed standardisation request, add a last-resort route for an urgent concern (internal market emergency mode, or suspended cooperation with international standardisation organisations with prior Council authorisation) that lasts only until the revised Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012 enters into force, move adoption to the examination procedure and let the Parliament object to common specifications, also under the Machinery, Batteries and Toy Safety Regulations. On digitalisation the EU declaration of conformity must be directly accessible, the digital contact freely accessible, and consumers may ask for paper instructions up to 24 months after purchase (professional users 6 months), sent within 15 working days; no term of the tracked concepts appears in it.

23 Apr 2026WK 5778/26Council: Presidency compromise textPresidency compromise - AGS on 27 April 2026
Our summary: Presidency note of 23 April 2026 to the Antici Group (Simplification), one joint document on both Omnibus IV proposals (this Regulation and the parallel Directive), offering compromises with the European Parliament on two open issues; a draft for discussion, not the Council's position and not law. On common specifications it proposes a narrowly framed last-resort power: where no harmonised standard exists or is expected, and to address the exceptional situation in which a lack of cooperation with international standardisation organisations prevents the European standardisation organisations from developing one, the Commission could adopt common specifications only after the Council has authorised it by implementing act, with a no-opinion clause in the committee and a time limit tied to the revision of Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012; in return the Council keeps its extension of the ecodesign transition and the deletion of the amendments to the Accreditation Regulation. On paper instructions it proposes one term, 'user', without the Parliament's split between consumers and professionals, a 24-month period to ask for paper instructions, and leaves open whether the reply is due within one month or 15 working days.

26 Jun 2026PE790.900Provisional agreementProvisional agreement resulting from interinstitutional negotiations Proposal for a regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council amending …
Our summary: Provisional agreement on the Omnibus IV Regulation on digitalisation and common specifications (COM(2025) 504), as the European Parliament's IMCO committee received it on 26 June 2026 for a single vote; an agreed text, not law, which still needs the plenary vote, the Council's adoption and legal-linguistic revision. Its wording is that of the Council's final compromise text ST 10482/26 ADD 1, except that one amendment to the conformity assessment annex of the Gas Appliances Regulation is missing. Compared with the Commission proposal it no longer amends Regulation (EC) No 765/2008, gives the Commission a last-resort power, limited to 48 months, to adopt common specifications without a prior standardisation request for cableways, personal protective equipment and gas appliances only, and moves the digital EU declaration of conformity and the other digital duties for machinery from 20 January 2027 to 30 months after entry into force. No term of the tracked concepts appears in it.
