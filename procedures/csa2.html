2026-05-13ST 9121/26council-progress-reportProgress report
Our summary: Progress report of the Cyprus Presidency of 13 May 2026 on CSA2 and the NIS-2 amendment, for Coreper and the Council to take note of; it is not a Council position. Member States generally support the proposal's objectives but ask for clarification of ENISA's new operational tasks, its resources and budget, and the certification deadlines. On the ICT supply chain framework they ask how key ICT assets and high-risk suppliers are identified and want more involvement of Member States; several entered scrutiny reservations, notably on governance, implementing acts and the high-risk supplier mechanism, and some asked the Council Legal Service to examine the legal basis.

No change to the findings. (source: ST 9399/26)

2026-08-07WK 11263/26council-compromisePresidency second compromise text Title III
Our summary: Presidency second compromise text of 7 August 2026 on Title III of the CSA2 proposal (European cybersecurity certification framework, Articles 71-97), following WK 7571/26; a draft, not law. It changes 42 provisions, mainly on how certification schemes are requested and prepared: feasibility studies by ENISA move from the recitals into Article 72, the ECCG can ask the Commission to request a candidate scheme (Article 73), ENISA has 12 months to prepare one unless the request sets another timeframe (Article 74), and the appeal against decisions of notified conformity assessment bodies becomes a separate Article 94a. Title IV (ICT supply chain security, high-risk suppliers) is reproduced unchanged from the Commission proposal, so the higher number of mentions of high-risk suppliers reflects a wider text, not a change.
