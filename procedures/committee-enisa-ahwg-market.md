## About

Tasks:

- The Agency is inviting experts with extensive knowledge and experience in cybersecurity market analysis and monitoring. Members will be expected to support ENISA in performing and disseminating regular analyses of key trends in the cybersecurity market, covering both the demand and supply sides, with the aim of fostering the cybersecurity market in the Union (Art. 8(7) CSA). They will also assist ENISA in conducting preparatory and supporting activities under the CRA.
