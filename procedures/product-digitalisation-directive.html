16 Feb 2026A10-0023/2026EP committee reportReport on the proposal for a directive of the European Parliament and of the Council amending Directives 2000/14/EC, 2011/65/EU, 2013/53/EU, 2014/29/EU, …
Our summary: Report of the European Parliament's Committee on the Internal Market and Consumer Protection (IMCO) on the Omnibus IV Directive, which amends thirteen sector directives (among them EMC, low voltage, radio equipment, ATEX and pressure equipment), tabled for plenary on 16 February 2026 with the opinion of the Environment committee annexed; it is the committee's position, not Parliament's and not law. Against the Commission proposal it narrows the Commission's new power to adopt common specifications: as a rule only where no harmonised standard exists or is expected and a standardisation request has failed, by examination procedure and with an expert group, plus a last-resort route, limited in time until the revised Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012 enters into force, for an internal market emergency or a suspension of cooperation with international standardisation organisations (the latter only after the Council's prior authorisation). On digital instructions it gives consumers 24 months and professional users 6 months to ask for paper instructions, shortens the reply to 15 working days, adds a telephone channel, and requires the declaration of conformity to be directly accessible and the manufacturer's digital contact to be freely accessible. A new recital explains the time limit by the forthcoming revision of Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012, to avoid overlap with future standardisation law.

23 Apr 2026WK 5778/26Council: Presidency compromise textPresidency compromise - AGS on 27 April 2026
Our summary: Presidency note of 23 April 2026 to the Antici Group (Simplification), one joint document on both Omnibus IV proposals (this Directive and the parallel Regulation), offering compromises with the European Parliament on two open issues; a draft for discussion, not the Council's position and not law. For the product directives amended by this Directive it offers model wording for a narrowly framed last-resort power on common specifications: only where no harmonised standard exists or is expected and a lack of cooperation with international standardisation organisations prevents the European standardisation organisations from developing one, only after the Council has authorised the Commission by implementing act, blocked by a no-opinion in the committee, and limited in time to after the revision of Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012. On paper instructions it proposes one term, 'user', a 24-month period to ask for them and leaves the reply period open between one month and 15 working days.

26 Jun 2026PE790.896Provisional agreementProvisional agreement resulting from interinstitutional negotiations Proposal for a directive of the European Parliament and of the Council amending Directives …
Our summary: Provisional agreement of June 2026 on the Omnibus IV Directive on digitalisation and common specifications, as submitted to the European Parliament's IMCO committee for a single vote; its wording is the same as the Council's final compromise text of 24 June 2026 submitted to Coreper for confirmation, and it is not yet law. Against the Commission proposal it keeps the electronic-only EU declaration of conformity and optional electronic instructions in thirteen product directives, but adds safeguards: safety information on paper or on the product where consumers may use it, a right to paper instructions up to 24 months after purchase, and a narrower "digital contact" that manufacturers and importers must show next to their postal address. The Commission may adopt common specifications only where harmonised standards are missing and a standardisation request has failed, by examination procedure, plus a time-limited last-resort route; the transposition period grows to 24 months and the digital rules apply after 30 months.
