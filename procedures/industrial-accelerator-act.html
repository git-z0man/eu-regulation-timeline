2 Sep 2026ST 12236/26 REV 1Council: Presidency compromise textSecond Presidency compromise text
Our summary: Second Presidency compromise text of 2 September 2026 (revision 1, with revised recitals) on the whole Industrial Accelerator Act proposal, prepared for the Council working party; a Presidency draft, not law, which can still change. Against the first partial compromise texts it moves the industrial acceleration areas into the permitting chapter and makes their designation optional, replaces the Commission's power to exclude third countries with lists of "partner origin" countries treated as Union origin, and redefines the scope of the foreign investment chapter around investors whose beneficial owners come from, or are controlled from, a country holding more than 40 % of global capacity in the sector. In the amendments to the Net-Zero Industry Act, the exclusion of high-risk suppliers identified under the proposed Cybersecurity Act 2 from control systems, SCADA, remote access systems and firewalls is gathered in one Article 28b for auctions, support schemes and State support to construction or manufacturing; the Presidency does not mark it as changed, and unlike the Commission proposal it names main specific components containing such systems explicitly.
Updated 13 Oct 2026: Reviewed after the original issue ST 12236/26 of 30 July 2026 was released and became this text's predecessor: its operative text is the same, REV 1 adds the recitals. "What it is", the comparison basis and "Tracked concepts" now say so; no finding about the articles and annexes changes, and the summary is unchanged. (source: ST 12236/26)

11 Sep 2026PE792.067EP draft report (rapporteur)DRAFT REPORT on the proposal for a regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council establishing a framework of measures for the acceleration of …
Our summary: Draft report of September 2026 by the three co-rapporteurs of the joint INTA, ITRE and IMCO committees on the Industrial Accelerator Act proposal, with 267 amendments; the rapporteurs' draft, not the position of the committees or of Parliament, and not law. It replaces the automatic treatment of content from free trade and procurement agreement partners as Union origin by an opt-in through delegated acts under seven cumulative conditions, with no such equivalence for public support schemes, and makes all six foreign investment conditions mandatory from EUR 50 million and a 10 % stake, also for subsidiaries of foreign investors. It raises the Union origin and low-carbon shares for steel, concrete, vehicles and net-zero technologies, raises the cost thresholds of the derogations to 40 %, adds social and labour conditions and drops the exclusion of digital technologies, AI, quantum and semiconductors from the sectors that may be added to the investment rules. The exclusion of high-risk suppliers from control systems, SCADA, remote access and firewalls in net-zero auctions and support schemes is not amended: it stays as the Commission proposed it, outside the auction cost derogation and applying to all auctions.
